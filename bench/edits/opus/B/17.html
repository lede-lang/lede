Most urgent status.sycamoretravel.example expires on 2026-10-30, in 14 days. Rotate it first.

Steps

  1. Request the new certificate in the certificate manager.
  2. Validate it via the DNS record.
  3. Wait for the status Issued.
  4. Do not remove the old certificate first Clients will get TLS handshake errors until the new certificate is attached. Attach the new one, then remove the old one later.
  5. Attach the new certificate to the listener on edge-alb-prod.
  6. Test with an external checker.
  7. Remove the old certificate after 24 hours.
Why this is manual for now The automated renewal job is broken until ticket EDGE-812 is fixed. Until then, rotate certificates with this runbook.
