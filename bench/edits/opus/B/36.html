Osprey Freight · Team charter

Data Reliability team

  • Effective 2026-11-01
  • Led by Grace Mbeki
  • 5 engineers

Mission

Make every dataset the business relies on trustworthy, on time and explained.

What we own

  • The freshness and quality checks on the 40 tier-1 tables.
  • The data incident process.
  • The lineage catalog.
  • The data contracts registry.

What we do not own

  • Dashboards and their business logic. Analytics owns them.
  • Source application databases. These are owned by product teams.
  • Ad-hoc data requests.

How we work

  • We plan and deliver in two-week sprints.
  • A rotating data on-call responds to tier-1 incidents within 15 minutes during business hours.
  • Every incident gets a written review within 5 business days.

How to reach us

Post in the #data-reliability channel, or file a ticket in the DREL queue.

Note: Requests sent by direct message will be redirected to the queue.
