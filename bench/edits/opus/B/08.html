Orders Platform Architecture

Kestrel Outfitters · Owner: Commerce Platform · Last reviewed 2026-10-01

Overview

The Orders platform takes web orders from customers, reserves stock in inventory, and charges cards through Stripe. At peak it handles about 3,200 orders per hour. Services run in a single production VPC, with order events fanned out over SQS so inventory updates stay decoupled from checkout.

Failure modes

Orders DB failover interrupts writes A Multi-AZ failover of Orders DB takes about 60 seconds. During that window order writes fail and the storefront shows a retry banner.
