Guardrails

Service control policies applied across the organization:

  • Deny leaving the organization.
  • Deny disabling CloudTrail.
  • Deny all regions other than us-west-2 and us-east-1.
  • Deny creating access keys for the root user.
Prod and Dev are isolated Prod and Dev VPCs cannot route to each other: the Transit Gateway uses separate route tables, rt-prod and rt-dev.
