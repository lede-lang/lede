Triage flow

Steps

  1. Check the consumer pods in the streaming namespace.
  2. Check per-partition lag for settlement-consumer.
  3. If one partition holds more than 40% of the total lag, treat it as partition skew.
  4. Scale the deployment to at most 24 replicas. The topic has 24 partitions, so extra replicas would sit idle.
Never reset consumer offsets to latest Unprocessed settlements would be skipped and would have to be reconciled by hand.
Escalation If lag is still growing after 30 minutes, escalate to the Streaming lead.
