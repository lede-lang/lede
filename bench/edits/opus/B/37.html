Welcome to Customer Success

Hello, and welcome!

We are so glad you are here. You are joining a team of 42 people across Denver, Dublin and Singapore, which means that somewhere in the world, a teammate is almost always awake and helping a customer get more out of Larkspur. We are a friendly, curious group, and we take our customers' success personally. This letter covers what your first days and months will look like, so you can focus on settling in.

Your first week

  • MondayLaptop pickup at 10:00, followed by a welcome breakfast.
  • TuesdayProduct deep dive with the Solutions team.
  • WednesdayShadow two customer calls.
  • ThursdaySupport tooling training.
  • FridayLunch with your onboarding buddy.

Your first 90 days

By day 30Your main job is to learn the product. Ask questions, sit in on calls and get comfortable with how our customers actually use Larkspur day to day.
By day 60You will own five accounts alongside your buddy. They will be there to help you prepare, follow up and handle anything unexpected.
By day 90You will run quarterly business reviews on your own. By then you will know your accounts well, and you will have plenty of support behind you.
