Replacing Jenkins: CI providers compared

Pros and cons

GitHub Actions

Pros

  • Hosted ARM runners
  • Supports self-hosted agents

Cons

  • Slowest median build at 14.2 min
  • $2,860 a month

CircleCI

Pros

  • Hosted ARM runners
  • Faster than GitHub Actions on every pipeline

Cons

  • Most expensive at $3,420 a month

Buildkite

Pros

  • Fastest median build at 9.6 min
  • Lowest license cost at $2,150
  • Self-hosted agents fit the hardware lab

Cons

  • ARM only via our own agents
  • We pay for our own compute
  • We patch the runners

Recommendation

Recommendation

Buildkite. The hardware-in-the-loop lab needs self-hosted agents anyway.

Warning

Buildkite moves runner patching onto the DevEx on-call, estimated at 6 hours a month.
