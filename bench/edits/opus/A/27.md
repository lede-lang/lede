# Feature Flags: Build or Buy

**Pebble Bank · Growth team**

Today we keep **140 flags** in a config file, and flipping a single flag takes a **40-minute deploy**. We compared three ways out.

## Comparison

| Option | Annual cost | Setup time | Targeting rules | Audit log |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LaunchDarkly | $54,000 | 1 week | Advanced | Yes |
| Unleash (self-hosted) | $9,000 hosting | 3 weeks | Good | Yes |
| In-house | No license cost | 10 weeks | Basic | Would need building |

## Recommendation

> **Choose LaunchDarkly.** It has the most advanced targeting rules, its audit log satisfies the compliance team, and it can be set up in 1 week, at $54,000 a year.

> **Note:** Finance approved the **$54,000** budget on **2026-10-14**.

## Risks

- None of the options has an SDK for our COBOL batch jobs.
