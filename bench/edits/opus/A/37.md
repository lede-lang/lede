# Welcome to Customer Success

**Larkspur Software · November 2026 cohort**

Hello, and welcome!

We're so glad you're here. You're joining a team of 42 people across Denver, Dublin and Singapore. Between us, the sun hardly ever sets on a Larkspur customer. We're the people customers turn to when they want to get more out of the product, and when something isn't working. This letter covers what your first days will look like, so you can spend less time wondering and more time settling in.

## Your first week

- **Monday:** Laptop pickup at 10:00, followed by a welcome breakfast.
- **Tuesday:** A product deep dive with the Solutions team.
- **Wednesday:** Shadow two customer calls.
- **Thursday:** Support tooling training.
- **Friday:** Lunch with your onboarding buddy.

## Your first 90 days

**By day 30**, your focus is learning the product. Use it the way our customers do, ask why things work the way they do, and keep notes on what confuses you. Those notes are often our best feedback.

**By day 60**, you'll own five accounts alongside your buddy. You'll lead the conversations, with someone experienced beside you to help with the tricky moments and share what they've learned.

**By day 90**, you'll run quarterly business reviews on your own. By then you'll know your customers well, and they'll know you.
