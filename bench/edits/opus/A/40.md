# Staff Password and Multi-Factor Authentication FAQ

**Cinder Valley School District · IT team · Policy takes effect 2026-11-03**

A new password and multi-factor authentication policy for all staff accounts takes effect on November 3, 2026. This page answers the most common questions about what changes and what you need to do.

> **Warning:** IT will never ask for your password or your code by email or phone. If anyone does, it's a scam: do not reply, and contact the help desk.

## Why are we changing the policy?

In 2026, two phishing incidents compromised staff mailboxes. Longer passwords and a second sign-in step make it much harder for a stolen password alone to give someone access to your account.

## What changes on November 3?

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters**.
- Every staff account needs **multi-factor authentication**.

## Do I have to change my password every 90 days?

No. We are removing forced rotation. We will only ask you to change your password after a suspected compromise.

## What if I do not have a smartphone?

The help desk will issue you a hardware key at no cost.

## What if I lose my phone or key?

Call the help desk at **extension 4410**. They will give you a temporary bypass code that is valid for 24 hours.

## Can I use the same password on other sites?

No. If another site is breached, attackers try the leaked password on other accounts, so a reused password could give them access to your district account.

## Who do I contact for help?

The help desk, open **07:00 to 17:00 on school days**, at extension 4410.
