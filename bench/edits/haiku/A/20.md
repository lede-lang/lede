# Postmortem: INC-3107 DNS misconfiguration

Network team, Alder Cloud Games. Incident date 2026-08-19.

| Duration | Lowest success rate | Matches failed | Region |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1 h 52 min** | **61.3%** | **48,200** | **EU-West** |

## What happened

A Terraform change replaced the weighted DNS records for `match.eu` with a single record. That record pointed at a decommissioned load balancer. Negative caching with a TTL of 3600 seconds kept failures going after the fix, so players kept seeing errors for up to an hour after the record was restored.
