# Build or buy: feature flags

The Growth team at Pebble Bank needs to decide whether to build or buy feature flags. Today we keep 140 flags in a config file, and a deploy takes 40 minutes just to flip one.

## Comparison

| Option | Annual cost | Setup time | Targeting rules | Audit log |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LaunchDarkly | $54,000 | 1 week | Advanced | Yes |
| Unleash (self-hosted) | $9,000 hosting | 3 weeks | Good | Yes |
| In-house | No license cost | 10 weeks | Basic | Would need building |

## Recommendation

> **Recommendation: LaunchDarkly.**
>
> LaunchDarkly covers our targeting needs with advanced rules, and its audit log satisfies the compliance team.
>
> **Note:** Finance approved the $54,000 annual budget on 2026-10-14.

## Risks

- None of the options has an SDK for our COBOL batch jobs.
