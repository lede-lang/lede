# Data Reliability team charter

Effective 2026-11-01. The Data Reliability team at Osprey Freight is led by Grace Mbeki and has five engineers.

## Mission

Make every dataset the business relies on trustworthy, on time and explained.

## What we own

- The freshness and quality checks on the 40 tier-1 tables.
- The data incident process.
- The lineage catalog.
- The data contracts registry.

## What we do not own

- Dashboards and their business logic. Analytics owns these.
- Source application databases. Product teams own these.
- Ad-hoc data requests.

## How we work

- We run two-week sprints.
- A rotating data on-call covers tier-1 incidents, with a 15-minute response time during business hours.
- Every incident gets a written review within 5 business days.

## How to reach us

- Post in the #data-reliability channel.
- Or open a ticket in the DREL queue.

> **Note: direct messages are redirected to the queue.**
> Requests sent by direct message will be redirected to the DREL queue, so please use the channel or the queue instead.
