# Welcome to Larkspur Software

Dear new colleague,

Welcome to Larkspur, and to the November 2026 cohort of the Customer Success team. We are glad you are here.

Our team has 42 people across Denver, Dublin and Singapore. We work closely with Solutions, Support and Enablement, and we care a great deal about the customers we serve. You will find people here who are generous with their time, and who want you to succeed.

## Your first week

- **Monday:** laptop pickup at 10:00, followed by a welcome breakfast.
- **Tuesday:** a product deep dive with the Solutions team.
- **Wednesday:** shadow two customer calls.
- **Thursday:** support tooling training.
- **Friday:** lunch with your onboarding buddy.

## Your first 90 days

**By day 30,** your goal is to learn the product. Spend time with the customers, the documentation and the people who build it. Ask questions freely.

**By day 60,** you will own five accounts, working alongside your onboarding buddy. You will lead the conversations, and your buddy will be there to review them with you.

**By day 90,** you will run quarterly business reviews on your own. By then you will know the accounts well, and you will know who to call when something is unclear.
