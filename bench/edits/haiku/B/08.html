Orders Platform Architecture

Owner: Commerce Platform team · Kestrel Outfitters · Last reviewed 2026-10-01

Overview

The Orders platform takes web orders, reserves stock and charges cards. It handles about 3,200 orders per hour at peak, and all order writes go through a single Multi-AZ database.

Architecture diagram

Components

ComponentRuntimePurpose
Orders ServiceECS Fargate, 6 tasksAccepts orders, reserves stock, charges the card and publishes order events
Stock ServiceECS Fargate, 4 tasksReserves and releases stock from order events
Orders DBRDS PostgreSQL 16 on db.r6g.xlarge, Multi-AZSystem of record for orders
Order EventsStandard SQS queue, 14 day retentionCarries order events to downstream consumers

Failure modes

Orders DB failover A Multi-AZ failover on Orders DB takes about 60 seconds. During that window order writes fail, and the storefront shows a retry banner.
