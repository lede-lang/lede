Clickstream Data Platform

Data Engineering · Harbor Media · As of 2026-09-30

Overview

We collect about 1,800 million events per day from our web and mobile apps. Events land in a data lake that analysts query through Redshift and Superset. Ingestion is batched through Firehose, and the nightly ETL on EMR turns raw events into curated tables.

Known limits

  • Firehose buffering means data arrives up to 5 minutes late.
  • The nightly ETL finishes around 03:40 UTC.
  • Redshift is a 4-node ra3.4xlarge cluster that queues queries above 50 concurrent users.
