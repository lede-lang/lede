Runbook

Manual failover of orders-aurora-prod

Owner: Database Reliability team · Fenwick Bank · Aurora PostgreSQL

When to use this runbook

Use this runbook in either of these cases:

  • The writer instance has been unhealthy for more than 5 minutes and automatic failover has not happened.
  • A planned failover during the Sunday maintenance window, 02:00 to 04:00 UTC.

Prerequisites

  • The break-glass role db-failover-operator.
  • Access to the #db-incidents channel.
  • The replica lag dashboard open.

Failover steps

  1. Announce the failover in #db-incidents.
  2. Confirm replica lag on the target reader is under 20 seconds.
  3. Put the orders service into read-only mode with the feature flag orders.readonly.
  4. Pause the nightly-reconcile job.
Stop: check lag before step 5 Never fail over to a reader whose lag exceeds 20 seconds, because committed orders will be lost.
  1. Trigger the failover to reader instance orders-aurora-prod-r2.
  2. Wait for the cluster endpoint to point at the new writer. This usually takes 30 to 60 seconds.
  3. Turn orders.readonly off.
  4. Watch the connection count recover to its normal level of about 400.

Rollback

If the new writer is unhealthy, fail back to orders-aurora-prod-r1 using the same steps. Then escalate to the on-call DBA lead.
