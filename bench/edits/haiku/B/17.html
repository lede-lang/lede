Steps

  1. Request the new certificate in the certificate manager.
  2. Validate it using the DNS record.
  3. Wait for the status to show Issued.
Attach the new certificate before removing anything Never remove the old certificate first. Clients will get handshake errors until the new one is attached.
  1. Attach the new certificate to the listener on edge-alb-prod.
  2. Test with an external checker.
  3. Remove the old certificate after 24 hours.
Why this is manual The automated renewal job is broken until ticket EDGE-812 is fixed. Until then, every rotation follows these steps by hand.
