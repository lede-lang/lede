Build or buy feature flags

Growth team, Pebble Bank. Decision page.

We keep 140 feature flags in a config file. Flipping one takes a full deploy, which currently takes 40 minutes. We need a flag system with targeting rules and an audit log. This page compares three options.

Recommended: LaunchDarkly LaunchDarkly covers our targeting needs with advanced rules and an audit log, and it has the shortest setup at one week. Finance approved the $54,000 annual budget on 2026-10-14.

Risks
