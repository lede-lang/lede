Observability stack for 2027

SRE, Copperleaf Insurance. Comparison for the budget meeting.

We run 340 hosts, ingest about 2.1 TB of logs a month, and have 14 engineers on call. We are comparing three options: Datadog, Grafana Cloud, and a self-hosted stack of Prometheus, Loki and Grafana on our own Kubernetes cluster.

Pros and cons

Datadog

  • Pro: excellent alerting and log search, and the lowest setup effort.
  • Con: the highest lock-in, and the highest annual cost.

Grafana Cloud

  • Pro: low setup effort, and our dashboards stay portable.
  • Pro: cheapest once staff time is counted.
  • Con: alerting and log search are good but not excellent.

Self-hosted

  • Pro: lowest lock-in and no license cost.
  • Con: high setup effort, and it depends on hiring 1.5 engineers.
  • Con: log search is fair at our volume.

Recommendation

We recommend Grafana Cloud. Once staff time is counted, it is the cheapest of the three over three years, and it keeps our dashboards portable.

Two dates and assumptions to note The Datadog quote expires on 2026-11-30. The self-hosted cost assumes we can hire the 1.5 engineers, which is not yet approved.
