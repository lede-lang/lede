Replacing Jenkins: choosing a CI provider

Developer Experience, Ridgeback Robotics. Covers the firmware and cloud repositories.

We run about 38,000 build minutes a month for 120 engineers. Some jobs need ARM runners, and the firmware pipeline needs a hardware-in-the-loop lab. We are comparing GitHub Actions, CircleCI and Buildkite.

Pros and cons

GitHub Actions

  • Pro: lowest monthly cost of the managed options, and it sits next to our code.
  • Pro: supports ARM runners and self-hosted agents.
  • Con: the slowest median build time of the three.

CircleCI

  • Pro: faster than GitHub Actions on every reference pipeline.
  • Pro: supports ARM runners and self-hosted agents.
  • Con: the highest monthly cost, at $3,420.

Buildkite

  • Pro: fastest median build time, at 9.6 minutes.
  • Pro: built around self-hosted agents, which the lab needs.
  • Con: ARM runners are only through our own agents.
  • Con: we would run the agents ourselves, and the cost is on top of our compute.

Recommendation

Recommended: Buildkite The hardware-in-the-loop lab needs self-hosted agents anyway, and Buildkite makes them required rather than optional. It was also the fastest on our reference pipelines.
Operational cost Buildkite moves runner patching onto the DevEx on-call rotation. We estimate this at 6 hours a month.
