Rainfall and yield

Each dot is one county. Across all four crops, the wetter the county, the higher its yield: the counties line up in the same order by rainfall as by yield.

Dashed lines mark the region averages. Five counties is a small sample, and rainfall is not the only difference between them, so treat this as a pattern, not proof of cause.
