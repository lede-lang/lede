Summary

The point-of-sale vendor added a column named tender_subtype to the sales feed without notice. The ingestion job failed on it. The failure alert went to a retired channel, so nobody knew until a store manager asked why the report was empty.

The reports were due at 03:00 UTC and went out at 11:40 UTC.

Contributing factors

  • The alert still routed to the retired channel #data-alerts-old.
  • There was no schema contract check on vendor feeds.
  • The backfill ran serially.

  • Once paged, the on-call found the cause in 45 minutes.
  • No wrong numbers were published.
