Marrow County Emergency Communications District

How a 911 call is received and dispatched

Owner: Public Safety Systems team · Reviewed 2026-09-30

Overview

Calls arrive from the telephone carriers into the primary dispatch center. A backup dispatch center across the county can take over if the primary is lost.

The backup center is never activated automatically. It is activated only when the shift supervisor declares the primary dispatch center unavailable.

Call path

live call or dispatch flow link to the backup center

CAD is the computer-aided dispatch system. Server A is active, Server B is its hot standby, and the CAD Replica in the backup center is fed asynchronously.

Failover targets

30 sec

Call routing switches to the Standby Call Router within 30 seconds.

2 min

CAD Server B takes over within 2 minutes.

< 5 sec

CAD Replica lags the active server by under 5 seconds.

10 min

Backup center is fully staffed and taking calls within 10 minutes.

Reminder: the first two targets cover failures inside the primary center. The 10-minute target applies only after the shift supervisor declares the primary unavailable; the backup center never starts on its own.
