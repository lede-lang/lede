What changed

  • Personal loans: highest rate, biggest rise. Delinquency went from 3.4% to 4.1%, up 0.7 points. At $143 million it is a small part of the book (8.7%).
  • Auto: slower rise, bigger dollars. Delinquency went from 1.9% to 2.2%, up 0.3 points. On $412 million of balances, that is roughly $9.1 million delinquent in Q3, against roughly $5.9 million for personal loans (approximate, from rounded rates).
  • Revolving credit: a small drift up. From 2.8% to 3.0%, up 0.2 points, on $96 million.
  • Mortgages: flat. At 0.6% in both quarters. They are 60.3% of balances, which keeps the portfolio rate low.
  • Losses and reserves. Charge-offs were $3.9 million in Q3 against $3.1 million in Q2. The allowance for loan losses is $21.3 million, 1.3% of loans.

Actions

  1. Tighten personal loan underwriting for credit scores below 640.
    Starting 2026-11-15
  2. Add early outreach for auto loans at 15 days past due.
    Auto: largest dollar exposure of the rising types
  3. Review reserve adequacy.
    At the January meeting
