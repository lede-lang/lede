Your MRI scan: what to expect

An MRI is painless, and our team looks after you the whole time. This page tells you what to do and what happens, step by step.

Please read first

If you have a pacemaker or any other metal implant, call us before the day. Call the imaging desk on extension 2210 at least 2 weeks ahead. It can mean we need to reschedule your scan, so please do not wait.

30 minArrive early
Level 2Imaging Department

1Before the day

Call the imaging desk on extension 2210 at least 2 weeks ahead if you have:

  • a pacemaker
  • a cochlear implant
  • an aneurysm clip
  • any other metal implant

Some devices need special planning.

  • Tell us if you might be pregnant or have kidney disease.
  • Do you feel uncomfortable in small spaces? Ask your doctor about a mild sedative. If you take one, arrange a driver.
  • Will your scan use contrast dye? Do not eat for 4 hours before. You can still drink water.

2On the day

Arrive 30 minutes early. Go to the Imaging Department on level 2.

Bring:

  • Photo ID
  • Your insurance details
  • The referral, if you were given a paper one

Leave in the locker: jewelry, watches, hearing aids and wallets.

What to wear: clothes without metal zippers or underwire.

3During the scan

  • It takes 30 to 60 minutes.You will be with us for a short time.
  • You lie on a padded bed.It slides into a tube that is open at both ends.
  • The machine is loud.We give you earplugs or headphones.
  • Keep very still.This gives us clear pictures.
  • You hold a squeeze ball.Squeeze it at any moment to call the technologist.

4After the scan

  • You can eat, drive and go back to work straight away. If you took a sedative, do not drive. Wait for your driver.
  • Your doctor receives the results within 3 business days.
  • The results appear in the patient portal after your doctor has seen them.
  • If you had contrast, drink extra water.
Questions before your scan? Call the imaging desk on extension 2210. St. Anselm Imaging, level 2.
