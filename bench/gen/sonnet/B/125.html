Month-End Close Runbook

  • For: accounting staff
  • Owner: Controller
  • Version 2026-10-01
  • Goal: books closed within 5 working days
Hard stop

The books lock at 12:00 on day 5. Nothing is posted after that without an approved reopen request.

Calendar

  1. Day1
    • Cut off sales and purchasing at 17:00.
    • Finish bank reconciliations.
  2. Day2
    • Post accruals and depreciation.
    • Reconcile inventory to the warehouse count.
  3. Day3
    • Eliminate intercompany balances between the three legal entities.
    • Reconcile payroll.
  4. Day4
    • Review variances.
  5. Day5
    • The Controller signs off.
    • The books lock at 12:00. LOCKED

Before sign-off

  • Every balance sheet account is reconciled.
  • Any reconciling difference is under 50 dollars or explained.
  • Variances over 5 percent and over 10000 dollars each have a written explanation.
  • Intercompany balances net to zero.
  • The trial balance debits equal credits.

If a deadline slips

  1. Tell the Controller the same day.
  2. Decide whether the slip delays sign-off. If day 5 will be missed by more than 1 working day, notify the CFO, Valentina Sokolov.
  3. Do not post late entries after the lock without a reopen request signed by the Controller.
  4. Record the cause in the close log.
