The system as a whole meets the target: 99.6 minutes per customer so far this year, against the regulator's 105 for the period.

Three districts are each above 105 minutes: Hills (176), Plains (131) and Coastal (112). Hills is the worst by a wide margin.

  1. Start the Hills tree-trimming program on 2026-11-02. It was approved with a budget of 1.8 million dollars and covers 210 line miles. Hills is the district furthest above target at 176 minutes.
  2. Keep Plains (131) and Coastal (112) under watch. Both remain above 105 minutes even though the system as a whole meets the target.
  3. Read Metro's result in context. Its 49 minutes reflects its underground cables, so it is a result of how the network is built, not a target other districts can compare themselves to directly.
