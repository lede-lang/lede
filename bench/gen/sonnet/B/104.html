Windspire Energy · Tallgrass wind farm

Telemetry pipeline architecture

Owner: Asset Performance · Reviewed 2026-10-06 · For the control room team

Overview

Turbine data is collected on site and summarized before it is sent over a limited link. The grid operator receives output and availability figures directly from the site.

Satellite fallback changes what you see.
  • When the site is on satellite fallback, the Control Room sees one-minute averages, not full-resolution data.
  • The Edge Historian can hold 72 hours of full-resolution data until the fiber link returns.

Pipeline

Heavy arrow: the only limited link in the pipeline. It is fiber in normal operation and satellite when fiber is down.
