Day 30legal response deadline from receipt of a valid request
+60 daysmaximum extension, complex requests only

Dashed zone: extension, up to 60 more days.

We must respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request.

The deadline can be extended by up to 60 more days, but only for complex requests, and only if we tell the requester within the first 30 days.

Our internal target is to deliver by day 25.

Handling steps

  1. Log the request in the privacy tracker within 1 business day

  2. Verify identity within 5 business days

    1. Ask for two documents.
    2. Never accept a photo of a card showing a full number.

  3. Confirm the scope with the requester by email

  4. Collect data from the six systems below

  5. Legal reviews the export and redacts other people's data

  6. Package

    1. Use an encrypted ZIP.
    2. Send the password by SMS, never in the same email.

  7. Deliver by day 25

    Record the delivery date.

  8. Close the ticket

    Keep the log for 3 years.

Backups are not searched. Note the date of the last backup instead.

Do not

  • Do not delete data in response to an access request.
  • Do not send the export to an address that was not verified.
  • Do not discuss the request in public channels.
