The lease total leaves out the optional 95,000 dollar buyout. Cash needed at the start: 412,000 dollars to buy, 15,300 dollars of deposit to lease.

Risks

If we buy

Buying ties up cash we need for the new bindery, and the resale value is a guess.

If we lease

The lease locks us in for 48 months, although the buyout price is fixed today.

Recommendation

Lease the press

  • It keeps the cash for the new bindery: 15,300 dollars of deposit instead of a 412,000 dollar purchase.
  • Its cost is fixed at 367,200 dollars with service included. The cost of buying depends on a resale value we can only guess; buying wins only if the press resells for more than 102,400 dollars.
  • If we want to own the press after 48 months, the buyout price is already fixed at 95,000 dollars.

The honest cost: the lease costs 17,600 dollars more than buying over four years, about 367 dollars more a month.
