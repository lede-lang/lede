A letter about Elm Street

To our customers, from Marguerite Pell

Dear friends,

We have some hard news, and we would rather you heard it from us than from a sign in the window. Here it is plainly, and then the rest of what we want to say.

What is changing

Our Elm Street shop, open for 17 years, will close for good after we trade on 23 December 2026.

Everything else carries on. Our Harbor Road shop, our online shop and our school book fairs all continue.

Why

Two things came together. The rent on Elm Street will rise 70 percent when the lease renews on 1 January 2027. And more than half of our sales now happen at Harbor Road or online.

We looked at those numbers for a long time. In the end, we chose to keep the two places that are growing. It was not an easy choice, and we did not make it lightly. We will miss Elm Street very much.

What stays the same

You can still find us at Harbor Road, online and at your child's school book fair. And you will find the same people.

  • Our booksellers. The Elm Street team of 6 booksellers will move to Harbor Road. The faces will be the same.
  • Gift certificates and store credit. We will honor them at Harbor Road and online until 31 December 2027.
  • Loyalty points. They move over automatically. There is nothing for you to do.

How to say goodbye

We would love to see you before we close. Here is what we have planned.

  • From 28 NovemberEverything in the Elm Street shop is 20 percent off.
  • Saturday 19 December, 11:00The last Saturday story hour.
  • 22 December, 17:00 to 20:00A farewell evening with our staff and local authors.
  • 23 DecemberOur last day of trading at Elm Street.

If you cannot make it, we understand. Come to Harbor Road, or order online, and tell us a story about the shop. We would like to hear it.

Thank you. For 17 years you have trusted us with your shelves, your gifts, your children's first books and your Saturday mornings. We have been lucky to be part of that, and we are grateful for every one of you.

With warmth and thanks,

Marguerite Pell
