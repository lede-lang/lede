How to read your water bill

Your bill has three parts: what you used, what it costs to take the water away, and a fixed charge. Here is what each one means.

Your bill at a glance

We send a bill every 3 months. Each bill adds up three things:

  • Usage charge: the water you used, measured in cubic meters (m3).
  • Sewer charge: 80 percent of your usage charge.
  • Fixed service charge: $38.40 every quarter, however much you use.

Example: a household that used 72 m3

Usage charges

We charge for water in three tiers. The more you use, the more each extra cubic meter costs. Each rate only applies to the water inside its tier.
