Walk-In Refrigerator Alarm

  • Owner: Operations Manager
  • Effective 2026-11-01
41°F
Use this page when the alarm sounds or the display reads above 41 degrees Fahrenheit.
Never

Never taste or sniff food to decide whether it is safe.

First two minutes

  1. Silence the alarm.
  2. Check that the door is fully closed.
  3. Read the display and the backup thermometer inside.
  4. Write the time on the log sheet.

Decide what to do

Move and call

41 to 45 degrees, for less than 2 hours

Above 41 for less than 2 hours, and 45 or below.

  • Move dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals into the backup chest freezers right away.
  • Keep produce in place.
  • Call the refrigeration technician.
Throw away

Above 45 degrees, or 2 hours or more, or unknown

Any one of these: above 45 degrees; above 41 for 2 hours or more; nobody knows how long.

  • Throw away dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals, with a second volunteer present to confirm.
  • Produce, canned and packaged goods can stay.

Steps in order

  1. Call ChillRight, the refrigeration vendor, on 555-0134 (open 24 hours) and ask for a 4-hour response.
  2. Message the Operations Manager.
  3. Move or discard items as above.
  4. Record each item thrown away on the waste log, with its weight in pounds.
  5. After the repair, run the refrigerator for 4 hours below 38 degrees before restocking.
