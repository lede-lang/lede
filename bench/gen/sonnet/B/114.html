Memphis and Tulsa plan

Memphis is forecast 2,200 parcels a day over capacity. Tulsa has only 2,400 of headroom. From 2026-11-23, we reroute 2,200 Memphis parcels a day to Tulsa.

The solid arrow is the plan. Omaha could take up to 7,200 more parcels a day, but rerouting there adds a day of transit.

Risks

  • No slack at Memphis or Tulsa. Any outage or weather delay at either depot during the window has nowhere to go except Omaha, which adds a day of transit.
  • The plan relies on all 640 seasonal workers starting by 2026-11-16. That is one week before the window opens on 2026-11-23. A late start puts the Memphis and Tulsa numbers at risk.
