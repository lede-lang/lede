Review times

StageTarget
Completeness checkwithin 2 business days
Fast-track reviewwithin 3 business days
Plan reviewwithin 15 business days
Heritage Board reviewApplies only to projects in a historic districtwithin 30 days

Historic districts take longer. The 30 days for Heritage Board review are added on top of the plan review time.

What that means for your path

  • 50000 dollars or lessCompleteness check, then fast-track review: 2 + 3 business days.
  • Over 50000 dollars, not in a historic districtCompleteness check, then plan review: 2 + 15 business days.
  • Over 50000 dollars, in a historic districtCompleteness check, Heritage Board review, then plan review: 2 + 15 business days, plus 30 days.

Fees

180dollars base fee, charged on every application
