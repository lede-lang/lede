About the exhibition

Tides of Trade follows the spice, tea and cotton routes that linked ports around the world between 1600 and 1850. Visitors move through the story by way of original ship ledgers, navigation instruments and a full-size reconstruction of a cargo hold.

The centerpiece is the 1783 log of the brig Hesper, shown publicly for the first time.

Dr. Rasmus Lindholm has curated 140 objects from 12 countries for the exhibition, which closes on May 9, 2027.
