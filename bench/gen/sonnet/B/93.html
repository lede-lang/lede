Alarm levels

Warning

Above 8 °C for 10 minutes

Critical

Above 12 °C or below 0 °C

Safe range: 2 to 8 °C.

First 15 minutes

  1. Acknowledge the alarm on the monitoring panel within 5 minutes.
  2. Check that the door is fully shut and the seal is intact.
  3. Check that the compressor has power, and note the temperature shown on the data logger.
  4. Call the refrigeration contractor Polar Service on 555-0190. They respond within 4 hours.

Moving stock

If the temperature is still above 8 °C after 15 minutes, move the doses.

Quarantine and escalation

Do not discard anything

Only the Quality Manager decides. No one else throws away, destroys or writes off a dose, whatever the logger says.

Any lot out of range for more than 30 minutes in total is quarantined, and the Quality Manager is told immediately.

If the alarm is not resolved within 2 hours, call the Operations Director.
