What each can and cannot do

Extended clinic hours

  • Handles any visit that needs an exam
  • Most expensive per contact, about $38
  • Fewest contacts, about 900 visits a month

Telehealth urgent care

  • About 1,500 visits a month, average wait 12 minutes
  • About $14 per contact
  • Cannot handle visits that need a physical exam

Nurse triage phone line

  • Answers within 3 minutes, about 2,200 calls a month
  • Vendor estimates it avoids 18 percent of emergency room referrals
  • A phone line, open 17:00 to 23:00 only, so it cannot examine a patient

Recommendation

Start with the telehealth service now and add the nurse line in the second half of 2027.

Telehealth reaches the most patients per dollar of the two options that can be live soon, with a 12-minute average wait.

Extended hours stays under consideration.

It is the most expensive option per contact, but it is the only one that treats patients who need an exam. If telehealth use is low, we revisit it.
