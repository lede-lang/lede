Borrowing at the library: your questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most. This page is by Hye-jin Park, circulation manager.

No more late fines from 1 November 2026.

Keep reading. We would rather have you back than have you worry.

Who can join the library?

Anyone who lives, works or studies in Millbrook County can join. Membership is free.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian needs to sign you up.

How many items can I borrow?

You can borrow up to 30 items at once. Within that:

30items in all
5DVDs at most
2Wi-Fi hotspots at most

How long can I keep things?

ItemLoan period
Books and audiobooks21 days
Wi-Fi hotspots14 days
DVDs7 days

What if I return something late?

There are no late fines from 1 November 2026. A day or two late costs you nothing.

Two things still apply:

  • An item that is 30 days overdue is treated as lost. We then charge its replacement cost, up to 25 dollars per item.
  • Borrowing is paused while your account owes 10 dollars or more.

Can't return something on time? Renew it, or ask us. We are glad to help.

How do I renew?

You can renew in three ways:

  • Online
  • In the app
  • At the desk

You can renew an item up to 3 times. The exception is an item another reader is waiting for. That one can't be renewed.

How do I reserve an item?

Reservations are free. You can have up to 15 at once.

When your item is ready, we email you. It waits for you at the desk for 7 days after that email.

Still have a question?

Email help@millbrooklibrary.example.

Or ask at the desk, on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00.
