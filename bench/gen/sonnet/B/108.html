What the trade-off means

Each wrongly blocked transaction is a customer declined at checkout. At this volume, every percentage point of wrongly blocked transactions is roughly 60,000 declined customers a month, so the block rate matters as much as the catch rate.

Vantage Shield versus Tollgate: its extra catch rate (3.6 points) comes with 1.2 points more wrongly blocked and $194,400 more in fees a year.

Recommendation

We lean toward Tollgate as the balanced choice.

It catches 91.2 percent of fraud, blocks 1.9 percent of good transactions and costs $295,200 a year. Kestrel Risk is the budget fallback if the steering group wants to cut fees by about $86,400 a year; it catches fewer fraud cases (89.5 percent) but blocks fewer good customers (1.2 percent).

Timing: sign by 2026-12-15 or lose the change-freeze window.

If the contract is signed by 2026-12-15, Tollgate and Kestrel Risk would be live before the change freeze on 2027-02-01. Vantage Shield, at 10 weeks, would not.
