Overview

Turbine readings flow from each farm into one central stream and are processed once. The processed data is then split into a fast store for dashboards and a cheap archive for audits.

If the uplink fails

6 hbuffer limit

The Edge Gateway keeps at most 6 hours of readings. Anything older is lost.

  1. The Edge Gateway buffers up to 6 hours of readings on disk.
  2. When the link returns, it replays them in order.
  3. After 6 hours, the oldest readings are dropped.

An outage longer than 6 hours means a permanent gap in the data. Check for it before trusting a trend.
