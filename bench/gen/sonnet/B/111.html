Watch items

Decision needed

Furniture: clearance

Furniture fell 32.1% from July (530) to September (360) as patio season ended. It is the only category that shrank across the quarter, and it needs a clearance decision.

Ask: decide whether to clear remaining patio stock now or carry it over. Please send your view to retail finance.

Seasonal and holiday: keep the momentum

Revenue rose from 90 in July to 520 in September after autumn displays went up on 2026-09-08. It was 23.2% of September revenue.

Plants: steady leader

Plants is the biggest category at 42.4% of the quarter (2,490 of 5,870) and had its best month in September at 910.
