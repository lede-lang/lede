Home-office stipend

Everything you need to know about getting help paying for your home workspace, in one place.

Effective January 1, 2027
  • Who is covered
  • What you get
  • What you can buy
  • How to claim
  • What is not covered

Who is covered

You are covered if you work at least 3 days a week from home and have completed 90 days of service.

Part-time employees receive 50 percent of the amounts on this page.

What you get

$500
One-time setup stipend
$40 a month
Toward your internet and phone

You can use the setup stipend again after 3 years.

What you can buy

  • Desk
  • Chair
  • Monitor
  • Keyboard
  • Headset
  • Lighting

How to claim

  1. Get approval if the purchase is over $250Purchases over $250 need written manager approval beforehand.
  2. Buy what you needKeep your receipt.
  3. Submit your receipts in the expense portalWithin 60 days of purchase.
  4. Get reimbursedYour reimbursement arrives within 10 business days.
Remember: ask your manager first for anything over $250. Approval after the purchase does not count.

What is not covered

  • Gaming equipment
  • Furniture for non-work use
  • Software subscriptions
  • Anything already supplied by the company
