Schedule fit

Each dot is a completion date. The line is the start of the school year. Green: finishes before it. Red: finishes after it. The chart starts on 2027-07-15.

Recommendation

Choose Copperfield Solar

  • It has the highest net benefit: $5,025,000, which is $265,000 above SunHarvest.
  • It has the earliest completion, 2027-07-31, which leaves 32 days before the school year starts.
  • It has a 25-year warranty and the largest system, 1,250 kW.
