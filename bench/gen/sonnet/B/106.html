A federal grant can cover up to 50 percent of capital cost.

Capital cost per rider

Capital cost divided by expected weekday riders. Lower is cheaper for each rider served.

Bars share one scale. Light rail costs about four times as much per rider as enhanced bus.

Trade-offs

Enhanced bus

Cheap and fast to deliver, but it carries the fewest riders and still shares congested lanes.

Bus rapid transit

The middle path. It carries many more riders than enhanced bus and could be upgraded to rail later.

Light rail

Carries the most riders and attracts development, but takes 8 years, and the city could not fund its share without a bond measure.

Recommendation

The department recommends bus rapid transit.

It balances cost, delivery time and ridership, and keeps the door open to rail later.
