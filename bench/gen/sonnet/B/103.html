How a finished lab result is routed

For new unit clerks and nurses · Current as of 2026-10-02

Routing flow

Start at the top. A critical value takes the URGENT path on the left, with a phone call and a read-back. Everything else takes the ROUTINE path on the right.

URGENT act now, phone-based, timed in minutes ROUTINE no phone call, timed in hours or days
