  • Completion is up 9 points in four terms, from 70.0% to 79.0%.
  • The biggest single jump, 5 points, came in Fall 2025, when free tutoring for first-year students started on 2025-08-25.
  • Online courses still complete about 18 points below in-person ones, so online is the format with the most room to improve.

Notes

What "completed" means
Completed means the student finished with a passing grade. It does not mean attendance.
Who counts as enrolled
Withdrawals before the census date, which is the 15th day of the term, are not counted as enrolled.
