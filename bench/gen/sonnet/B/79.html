Operations effort, hours a month

Risks

Managed Kafka: team experience

Only 2 of our 9 engineers have run Kafka before, so we budget 5 days of training.

SQS with SNS: no replay

Replay of 7 days is not available, so we would have to build and run an archive to storage ourselves to meet the audit requirement. The 3,000 messages per second cap on each FIFO queue means two queues for our 4,200 at peak.

Self-run RabbitMQ: effort and unfamiliar parts

It needs 30 hours of operations a month, we would build ordering ourselves, and replay depends on the streams plugin, which the team has not used.

Recommendation

Choose Managed Kafka

  • It is the only option that meets both ordering per account and 7-day replay without extra building.
  • Replay is an audit requirement, and SQS with SNS cannot provide it.
  • We budget 5 days of training to cover the experience gap: 2 of 9 engineers have run Kafka.

The honest cost: Managed Kafka costs $3,750 a month more than SQS with SNS, which is $45,000 a year more ($70,200 against $25,200).
