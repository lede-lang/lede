# Vantage Outdoor Gear: 2026 revenue by region, January to September

This page summarizes revenue by region for Vantage Outdoor Gear from January to September 2026. All figures are in thousands of dollars.

## Headline figures

- **Year-to-date revenue:** $13,567K
- **Best month:** September, at $1,712K
- **APAC growth, January to September:** 44.8 percent
- **Americas share:** 45.2 percent

> **Note: figures are unaudited.**
> These figures are unaudited and exclude the wholesale channel.
