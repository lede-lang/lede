# Choosing a frontend framework for the scheduling admin app

The Internal Tools guild at Wrenfield University IT is rewriting the course-scheduling admin app. The app has about 60 screens, five developers, and is mostly forms and tables. This page compares three frameworks for the rewrite.

## Criteria

- Team familiarity
- Form and table libraries
- Hiring pool
- Bundle size
- Long-term support

## Comparison

| Framework | Team familiarity | Form/table ecosystem | Bundle size (KB) | Hiring pool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| React | 4 of 5 developers | Excellent | 142 | Large |
| Vue | 1 of 5 developers | Good | 98 | Medium |
| Svelte | 0 of 5 developers | Fair | 41 | Small |

## Pros and cons

### React

- Pros
  - Four of the five developers already know it.
  - The data grid we need exists today.
  - Large hiring pool.
- Cons
  - Largest bundle of the three.

### Vue

- Pros
  - Smaller bundle than React.
  - Good form and table ecosystem.
- Cons
  - Only one developer knows it.
  - Medium hiring pool.

### Svelte

- Pros
  - Smallest bundle, at 41 KB.
  - Favored in the prototype spike.
- Cons
  - Nobody on the team knows it yet.
  - Fair ecosystem for forms and tables, and a small hiring pool.

## Recommendation

> **Recommendation: React.**
>
> Four of the five developers already know it, and the data grid we need exists today.

Hannah Osei, the guild lead, put the priority plainly:

> "We are optimizing for the next five years of maintenance, not the first five weeks of building."

Svelte was the favorite in the prototype spike. We will revisit it for smaller tools.
