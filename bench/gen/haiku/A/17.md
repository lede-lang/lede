# Runbook: manual TLS certificate rotation on load balancers

Edge team, Sycamore Travel.

## Steps

1. Request the new certificate in the certificate manager.
2. Validate it through the DNS record.
3. Wait for the status to show Issued.

> **Warning: never remove the old certificate first.** Clients will get handshake errors until the new certificate is attached.

4. Attach the new certificate to the listener on `edge-alb-prod`.
5. Test with an external checker.
6. Remove the old certificate after 24 hours.

> **Note:** The automated renewal job is broken until ticket EDGE-812 is fixed. That is why this rotation is manual for now.
