# Staff password and multi-factor authentication: FAQ

The IT team at Cinder Valley School District is introducing a new password and multi-factor authentication policy for staff. It takes effect on 2026-11-03. This page answers the most common questions.

> **Warning:** IT will never ask for your password or your code by email or phone. If someone does, do not respond, and report it to the help desk.

## Why are we changing the policy?

Two phishing incidents in 2026 compromised staff mailboxes. The new policy makes accounts harder to take over.

## What changes on November 3?

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Every staff account needs multi-factor authentication.

## Do I have to change my password every 90 days?

No. We are removing forced password rotation. We will only ask you to change your password after a suspected compromise.

## What if I do not have a smartphone?

The help desk will issue you a hardware key at no cost.

## What if I lose my phone or key?

Call the help desk at extension 4400. They will issue a temporary bypass code, which is valid for 24 hours.

## Who do I contact for help?

Contact the help desk. It is open from 07:00 to 17:00 on school days.
