Your MRI scan: what to expect

St. Anselm Imaging. Read this before your scan. It explains what to do before the day, on the day, during the scan and after it.

Pacemaker or any metal implant? Please call the imaging desk on extension 2210 at least 2 weeks before your scan. Some devices need special planning, and this can mean rescheduling, so call early.

Before the day

  • If you have a pacemaker, a cochlear implant, an aneurysm clip or any other metal implant, call the imaging desk on extension 2210 at least 2 weeks ahead.
  • Tell us if you might be pregnant or have kidney disease.
  • If you feel uncomfortable in small spaces, ask your doctor about a mild sedative. If you take one, arrange for someone to drive you.
  • If your scan uses contrast dye, do not eat for 4 hours before. You can still drink water.

On the day

  • Arrive 30 minutes early at the Imaging Department on level 2.
  • Bring a photo ID, your insurance details, and your referral if you were given a paper one.
  • Leave jewelry, watches, hearing aids and wallets in the locker.
  • Wear clothes without metal zippers or underwire.

During the scan

The scan takes 30 to 60 minutes. You will lie on a padded bed that slides into a tube open at both ends.

The machine is loud, so we give you earplugs or headphones.

You will need to keep very still. You will hold a squeeze ball. Squeeze it at any moment to call the technologist.

After the scan

  • You can eat, drive and go back to work straight away, unless you took a sedative.
  • If you had contrast dye, drink extra water.
  • Your doctor receives the results within 3 business days. They appear in the patient portal after your doctor has seen them.
