How a return request gets decided

Greenmoor Apparel customer care · current as of 2026-10-12

Decision flow

Service levels

StepTarget
Label issuedWithin 1 business hour of request
Item inspectionWithin 2 business days of arrival
Refund postedWithin 3 business days of inspection
Returns Lead reviewWithin 1 business day

Exceptions

  • Damaged-on-arrival items skip the 30-day check and the final-sale check.
  • Gift returns always get store credit.
  • Customers with more than 4 returns in 90 days are routed to the Returns Lead.
