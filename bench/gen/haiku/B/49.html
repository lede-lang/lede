Shipment webhooks: delivery, retries and failure

Parcelwise integrations documentation · version dated 2026-10-05

Delivery sequence

A response slower than 10 seconds counts as a failure. The delivery is retried on the schedule below, even if your endpoint processed the event after the timeout.

Retry schedule

AttemptDelay after the previous failure
1Immediate

52 hours
612 hours

After attempt 6 fails, the event is parked in the Dead Letter Queue.

Good to know

  • Every request carries an X-Parcelwise-Signature header, which is the HMAC-SHA256 of the request body.
  • Events can arrive out of order. Compare the sequence number, not the arrival order.
  • Parked events are kept for 14 days. You can replay them from the dashboard.
