Net benefit

Net 25-year benefit is 25-year savings minus price. Bars start at zero.

Schedule fit

Three bids finish before the school year starts on 2027-09-01: Copperfield (2027-07-31), SunHarvest (2027-08-15) and Meridian (2027-08-31).

Brightline (2027-09-30) and Halcyon (2027-10-31) would finish after the school year starts. Their panels would not be generating for the start of term.

Recommendation

We recommend Copperfield Solar. It has the highest net benefit, at 5,025,000 dollars, which is 265,000 dollars above SunHarvest. It also has the earliest completion date, 2027-07-31, and a 25-year warranty.

Copperfield also has the highest upfront price, at 2,875,000 dollars. The district must finance that cost.
