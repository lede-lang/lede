What changed

  • Personal loans have the highest delinquency at 4.1 percent and the largest rise, up 0.7 points.
  • Auto loans are rising more slowly, up 0.3 points, but they matter more in dollars because the balance is $412 million.
  • Mortgages are 60.3 percent of balances and were flat at 0.6 percent.
  • Charge-offs rose to $3.9 million in Q3 from $3.1 million in Q2.

Actions

  • Tighten personal loan underwriting for credit scores below 640, starting 2026-11-15.
  • Add early outreach for auto loans at 15 days past due.
  • Review reserve adequacy at the January meeting.
