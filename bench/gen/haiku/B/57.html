Risks

Pipeboard

The ERP connector is certified, but the vendor is a 2019 startup.

Cardinal CRM

It is the best fit for our workflow, but it needs a 12-week implementation and a partner.

Thistle

It is the cheapest, but its reporting is weak, and two of our dashboards would have to be rebuilt elsewhere.

Recommendation

We go with Pipeboard. It has the highest weighted total, at 4.05, and it is certified for our ERP. Its list price is $40,320 a year less than Cardinal CRM.

This is pending a reference call with another industrial distributor, scheduled for 28 October 2026.
