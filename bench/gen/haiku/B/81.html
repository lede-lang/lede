Hollis Public Library · Summer 2026

A summer of stories, 8,325 books strong

Our summer reading program ran from June to August 2026. Here is how the season went, from the first book logged to the closing party.

Summer at a glance

1,248children and teens registered
683finished the program (54.7 percent)
8,325books logged in total
6.7books per registered reader
310people at the closing party on September 12

Thank you to every family, volunteer and teacher who helped make the summer happen. July was our busiest month for reading, and the finishers are the readers we are proudest of.

Books logged by month

Total: 8,325 books across the three months.

Readers by age group

Of our 1,248 registered children and teens, 402 were under 8, 561 were 8 to 12, and 285 were 13 to 17. The 8 to 12 group was the largest.
