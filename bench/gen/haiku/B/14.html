Real-Time Quote Pricing Model

ML Platform team · Orchard Insurance · Reviewed 2026-10-12

Overview

The service prices about 450 quotes per second at peak, with a p99 target of 250 ms. Agents submit quote requests through the Agent Portal, and the Inference VPC scores each one against the approved model.

Deployment

  1. Register the model in the Model Registry.
  2. Approve it in the Model Registry.
  3. Blue/green deploy, shifting 10 percent of traffic every 5 minutes.
  4. Roll back automatically if p99 exceeds 250 ms.
The Feature Cache is not a source of truth A cache miss falls back to the policy database and adds about 90 ms to the request.
