Parental leave: frequently asked questions

Our new parental leave policy takes effect on 1 November 2026. This page answers the questions we hear most often.

How much leave do I get?

Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of leave at full pay. Secondary caregivers get 8 weeks at full pay. This is the same whether you are welcoming a child by birth, adoption or foster placement.

Who is eligible?

The policy applies to employees who have worked at Tessellate for at least 6 months on the day their leave starts.

When can I start and finish?

You can start your leave up to 2 weeks before the expected date. You have to finish it within 12 months of the birth or placement.

Can I split my leave?

Yes. You can take your leave in at most two blocks. The second block needs to be agreed with your manager at least 4 weeks ahead.

What happens to my pay, benefits and equity?

During the full-pay weeks described above, you are paid in full. Your health insurance continues at the normal employer contribution, and your equity keeps vesting while you are on leave.

How do I come back?

When you return, you can work a transition schedule of 80 percent time at 100 percent pay for the first 4 weeks.

How do I apply?

Tell your manager and the People Team at least 30 days ahead. If your child is arriving early, tell us as soon as you can. Use the leave form in the HR portal.

Questions go to leave@tessellate.example or to the #people-help channel.

We hope this makes planning easier for you and your family.
