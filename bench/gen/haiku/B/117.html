Corbel & Finch Books

A letter about our Elm Street shop

What is changing

After 17 years, we are closing our Elm Street shop for good. It will trade for the last time on 23 December 2026. We know many of you have bought your first books, your children's picture books and your gifts in those rooms, and we are sorry to say goodbye to them.

Our Harbor Road shop, our online shop and our school book fairs all continue as before.

Why

When the Elm Street lease renews on 1 January 2027, the rent would rise by 70 percent. We also looked honestly at where you are buying from us. More than half of our sales now happen at Harbor Road or online. So we chose to keep the two places that are growing, and to let the one that is not go.

What stays the same

Your gift certificates and store credit will be honored at Harbor Road and online until 31 December 2027. Your loyalty points move over to us automatically, so there is nothing you need to do.

Our Elm Street team of six booksellers will move to Harbor Road. You will still see the same faces, and we will still know your reading tastes.

  • From 28 November 2026: all stock at Elm Street is 20 percent off.
  • 19 December 2026, 11:00: our last Saturday story hour.
  • 22 December 2026, 17:00 to 20:00: a farewell evening with our staff and local authors.

How to say goodbye

Please come and see us before the end. Browse the shelves, bring a friend, and tell us what the shop has meant to you. We would love to hear your stories, whether you have been coming since the first year or since last week.

Thank you. Thank you for the conversations, the recommendations, the book clubs and the children who read to us. You made the last 17 years possible, and you will keep the shop's spirit going at Harbor Road and online.

With gratitude,

Marguerite Pell

Owner, Corbel & Finch Books
