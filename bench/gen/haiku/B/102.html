Sale-to-Stock Sequence

Owner: Retail Platform team · Dated 2026-10-08 · For new engineers

This page explains how a sale at a store register reaches the online stock count.

Sale-to-stock sequence

Good to know

The Stock Ledger is the only source of truth. The Storefront count is a copy and may lag the ledger by up to 20 seconds.

  • The local queue on the edge holds up to 4 hours of sales, which is about 3,000 adjustments at a busy store.
  • Every adjustment carries an idempotency key, so replays never double count a sale.
  • Online orders reserve stock for 15 minutes at checkout.
