Fraud Screening Vendor Comparison

Fennimore Payments · For the payments steering group · Decision needed by 2026-11-20 · Risk lead: Gideon Ashworth

We process about 6 million customer payment transactions a month and need to choose a fraud-screening vendor by 2026-11-20. We lean toward Tollgate as the balanced choice.

Annual fees

At 6 million transactions a month, that is 72 million a year.

What the trade-off means

Each wrongly blocked transaction is a customer declined at checkout.

Vantage Shield catches 3.6 points more fraud than Tollgate. That comes with 1.2 points more good transactions wrongly blocked, and $194,400 more in fees a year.

Kestrel Risk is the budget option. It costs $86,400 a year less than Tollgate, but catches 1.7 points less fraud (89.5% against 91.2%).

Recommendation

We lean toward Tollgate as the balanced choice between fraud caught, customers declined and fees. Kestrel Risk is the budget fallback if the steering group wants to cut about $86,400 a year in fees.

Timing: if the contract is signed by 2026-12-15, Tollgate (6 weeks) and Kestrel Risk (4 weeks) would be live before the change freeze on 2027-02-01. Vantage Shield, at 10 weeks, would not.
