Borrowing FAQ

Millbrook Public Library

New from 1 November 2026: we no longer charge late fines. Items can still become overdue, so read the section on late returns below.

Who can join the library?

Membership is free. You can join if you live, work or study in Millbrook County.

Children under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign them up.

How many items can I borrow?

You can borrow up to 30 items at once.

  • No more than 5 of them can be DVDs.
  • No more than 2 of them can be Wi-Fi hotspots.

How long can I keep things?

  • Books and audiobooks: 21 days.
  • DVDs: 7 days.
  • Wi-Fi hotspots: 14 days.

What if I return something late?

There are no late fines from 1 November 2026.

But if an item is 30 days overdue, we treat it as lost. You will be charged its replacement cost, up to $25 for each item.

While your account owes $10 or more, you cannot borrow anything new. Pay the balance to start borrowing again.

How do I renew?

You can renew online, in the app, or at the desk.

You can renew an item up to 3 times, unless another reader is waiting for it.

How do I reserve an item?

Reservations are free. You can have up to 15 reserved items at once.

When your item is ready, we email you. It then waits for you at the desk for 7 days.

Questions? Email help@millbrooklibrary.example or visit the desk on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00.
