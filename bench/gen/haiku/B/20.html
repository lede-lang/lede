What happened

A Terraform change replaced the weighted DNS records for match.eu with a single record. That record pointed at a decommissioned load balancer, so matchmaking requests for players in Europe failed. Negative caching with a TTL of 3600 seconds then kept the failures going after the bad record was restored, because resolvers kept the failed answer for up to an hour.

Action items

  • Require a plan review for DNS changes.
  • Lower the negative cache TTL to 300 seconds.
  • Add synthetic matchmaking checks from three EU cities.
Until the negative cache TTL is lowered, a DNS fix can take up to an hour to reach players.
