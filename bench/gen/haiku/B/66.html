Release notes

Lantern Notes 3.8.0

Released 14 October 2026

Shared folders, scheduled dark mode and Evernote import are here, along with a faster search and a list of fixes.

New

  • Shared folders. Invite up to 25 collaborators to a folder, and choose view or edit access for each one.
  • Scheduled dark mode. Dark mode can follow sunset, or a time range you set.
  • Import from Evernote. Bring in your ENEX files, with attachments up to 50 MB each.

Improved

  • Faster search. Search is about twice as fast. On a library of 10,000 notes, the median query dropped from 410 ms to 190 ms.
  • Better battery life on Android. Background sync uses about 18 percent less battery.

Fixed

  • Tables keep their column widths when you export to PDF.
  • The app no longer freezes when you open a note with more than 200 images.
  • Reminders fire at the right time after a daylight saving change.
  • Pasted web links keep their titles.
  • Dragging a note onto a folder in the sidebar works with a trackpad on Windows.
  • The word count now includes text inside tables.

Known issues

  • Shared folders cannot yet be used with end-to-end encrypted notebooks. This is planned for 3.9.
  • Evernote import drops nested tags beyond three levels.

Deprecations

  • Windows 8.1 support ends on 31 January 2027.
  • From version 3.9, the minimum Android version will be Android 10.
