Lost or stolen laptop: response card

Wexford Legal IT · effective 2026-11-01 · owner: IT Security

Report it within 15 minutes of noticing.

Call the service desk on extension 4100. Do not report by email.

A lost laptop is not automatically a data breach. Full-disk encryption is enforced on all laptops. It must still be reported.

What the service desk does

  1. Mark the device as lost in the device manager. This locks it and starts a remote wipe on its next connection.
  2. Disable the user's sessions and revoke their VPN certificate.
  3. Check whether the laptop held client files outside the document management system. This is not allowed, but it does happen.
  4. If it did, notify the General Counsel's office within 1 hour. Client confidentiality rules may require telling the client.
  5. Order a replacement. Standard turnaround is 1 business day.
  6. File the report in the incident log within 24 hours.
