Cold Room B temperature alarm

Marlowe Vaccine Depot · Owner: Depot Operations

Cold Room B holds 12,400 doses and must stay between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

Nobody discards anything. Only the Quality Manager decides.Do not throw away, relabel, or destroy any stock, even if it has been out of range.

Alarm levels

WARNINGTemperature above 8 degrees for 10 minutes.
CRITICALTemperature above 12 degrees, or below 0 degrees.

First 15 minutes

  1. Acknowledge the alarm on the monitoring panel within 5 minutes.
  2. Check the door. Confirm the door is fully shut and the seal is intact.
  3. Check the compressor. Confirm it has power, and note the temperature shown on the data logger.
  4. Call Polar Service on 555-0190. The refrigeration contractor responds within 4 hours.

Moving stock

If the temperature is still above 8 degrees after 15 minutes:

  • Move the doses to backup Cold Room C, which holds 9,000 doses.
  • The remaining 3,400 doses go into the 6 validated transport boxes with ice packs.
  • Log every box on the transfer sheet.

Quarantine and escalation

  • If any lot is out of range for more than 30 minutes in total, quarantine that lot and tell the Quality Manager immediately.
  • Do not discard anything. Only the Quality Manager decides what happens to quarantined stock.
  • If the alarm is not resolved within 2 hours, call the Operations Director.
