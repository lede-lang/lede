Brightwater Maritime Museum opens “Tides of Trade”

A new exhibition of 140 objects from twelve countries traces the trade routes that shaped the world between 1600 and 1850.

About the exhibition

“Tides of Trade” follows the spice, tea and cotton routes between 1600 and 1850. Visitors meet these journeys through original ship ledgers, navigation instruments and a full-size reconstruction of a cargo hold, so the trade can be seen from the deck as well as from the desk.

The centerpiece is the 1783 log of the brig Hesper, which is shown publicly for the first time.
