Watch items

Decision needed: Furniture clearance. Furniture fell 32.1% from July ($530k) to September ($360k) as patio season ended. It is the one category that needs a clearance decision.

Plants remain the largest category at 42.4% of the quarter, so they carry most of the revenue risk if demand softens.
