Who each plan suits

Basic

Employees who want the lowest premium and do not expect orthodontia.

Plus

Employees who want a higher annual maximum and a lower deductible than Basic, with no orthodontia.

Premier

Employees who want orthodontia coverage at a moderate price. In the pulse survey, 38 employees said they have children who will need braces soon.

Family Max

Families with ongoing orthodontia, which need the higher lifetime maximum and no deductible.

Recommendation

We recommend Premier as the default plan. It covers orthodontia at 50 percent, with a $25 deductible, for $492 a year.

Family Max costs $264 more a year. It only pays off for families with ongoing orthodontia that need more than the $1,500 lifetime maximum on Premier.
