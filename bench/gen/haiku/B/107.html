Spring 2027 Fundraising Event

Summit Youth Mentoring · Board meeting 2026-11-17 · From Priya Venkataraman, Development Director

We need to choose our main fundraising event for spring 2027. The goal is to net at least $100,000. Our recommendation is the spring gala, with the online campaign as a low-cost add-on.

Net revenue range

Each bar runs from the low to the high estimate. The dashed line is the $100,000 goal.

Risks

Gala: the whole range stays above the goal, but it depends on a few large sponsors and needs the most money up front.

Walkathon: reaches the goal only at the top of its range, and is exposed to weather on the day.

Online campaign: never reaches the goal on its own, and depends on social media reach.

Recommendation

We recommend the spring gala, with the online campaign as a low-cost add-on.

The gala is the only option whose full range stays above the goal. It also needs the most money up front and the most effort.

The board needs to approve the $60,000 gala budget on 2026-11-17, for a gala date of 2027-04-24.
