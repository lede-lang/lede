Pros and cons

Harbor Point

For: lab-ready now, so no fit-out and no second site. The shortest median commute, at 24 minutes. A move-in on 1 February 2027 leaves two months before the current lease ends.

Against: the highest rent, $288,000 a year more than Mill District. A 10-year commitment.

Mill District

For: $288,000 a year less in base rent than Harbor Point. Partly lab-ready, in one building with the rest of the team.

Against: about $1.1 million of fit-out. A 7-year term gives us less time to recover that money than the 10-year options. A 1 March 2027 move-in leaves only a month before the current lease ends on 31 March. The median commute is 31 minutes.

Northgate Park

For: the lowest rent, $192,000 a year below Mill District. The earliest move-in, on 15 January 2027.

Against: no lab space, so we would need a separate lab lease nearby. It splits the team across two sites. It has the longest median commute, at 42 minutes.

Recommendation

Our leaning is Mill District. Harbor Point costs $288,000 a year more in base rent, and Northgate Park would split the team across two sites, with a separate lab lease and the longest commute.

Mill District has real downsides. The fit-out of about $1.1 million is a large sunk cost, the 7-year term gives us less time to recover it, and a 1 March 2027 move-in leaves little margin before the current lease ends on 31 March 2027. We think those risks are worth taking for the saving, but the leadership team should see them plainly on 13 November 2026.
