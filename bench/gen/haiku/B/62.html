What is changing

  • The Library completed an LED retrofit in October 2025.
  • An HVAC controls upgrade is planned for Science Hall and the Engineering Annex. It is budgeted at $640,000 for FY2027 and is expected to save about 9 percent in those two buildings. On FY2026 use, that is roughly 560 MWh a year, based on their combined 6,260 MWh.
