Charter

Windrow Software Customer Advisory Council

Effective 1 January 2027

Sponsored by Ines Carvalho, Chief Product Officer

Purpose

The council gives customers a structured, regular way to shape the product roadmap. It also gives them a way to tell us plainly what is not working.

Membership

The council has 14 seats: 12 customer members and 2 Windrow product leads. Customer members are chosen to reflect our customer base by size and industry.

  • Customer members serve two-year terms, renewable once.
  • Half of the customer seats rotate each year, to keep continuity.
  • The chair is Ines Carvalho.
  • The vice chair is elected by the customer members.

Meetings

The council meets quarterly for two hours. Meetings are held on 10 March 2027, 9 June 2027, 8 September 2027 and 8 December 2027.

  • An agenda is sent 10 days before each meeting.
  • Written minutes are shared within 5 business days.
  • A quorum is 8 members, including at least 6 customers.

What the council does and does not do

The council does

  • Reviews roadmap themes
  • Tests early designs
  • Ranks customer-submitted requests

The council does not

  • Approve pricing
  • Commit Windrow to a delivery date
  • Replace the normal support channel

Confidentiality

All members sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before the first meeting. Roadmap material shown to the council is confidential unless it is marked otherwise.
