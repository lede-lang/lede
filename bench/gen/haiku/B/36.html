Data Reliability team charter

Effective 2026-11-01. Led by Grace Mbeki, with 5 engineers.

Mission

Make every dataset the business relies on trustworthy, on time and explained.

What we own

  • The freshness and quality checks on the 40 tier-1 tables.
  • The data incident process.
  • The lineage catalog.

What we do not own

  • Dashboards and their business logic. Analytics owns these.
  • Source application databases. Product teams own these.
  • Ad-hoc data requests.

How we work

  • We work in two-week sprints.
  • Data on-call rotates across the team. During business hours, we respond to tier-1 incidents within 30 minutes.
  • Every incident gets a written review within 5 business days.

How to reach us

Use the #data-reliability channel for questions and discussion. For work that needs tracking, open a ticket in the DREL queue.

Note Requests sent by direct message will be redirected to the DREL queue.
