Message backbone for the settlement service

Fernhill Payments · For Ewan Calloway, platform lead · Architecture review on 2026-11-09

Requirements

  • 4,200 messages per second at peak
  • 6 consuming services
  • Ordering per customer account
  • Replay of the last 7 days, for audits

Risks

Team experience

Only 2 of our 9 engineers have run Kafka before. We budget 5 days of training.

Cost

Kafka is the most expensive option, at $3,750 a month more than SQS with SNS.

Recommendation

We recommend Managed Kafka.

It costs $3,750 a month more than SQS with SNS, but replay is an audit requirement, and only Kafka has it built in. The main risk is the team's limited experience with Kafka, so we budget 5 days of training.
