Welcome to Larkspur Customer Success

Dear new colleague,

Welcome, and congratulations on joining the November 2026 cohort. You are joining a team of 42 people across Denver, Dublin and Singapore. We are a mix of account managers, support engineers, and enablement specialists, and we share one goal: customers who get more value from Larkspur every quarter. We are glad you are here.

Your first week

  • Monday: laptop pickup at 09:00, then a welcome breakfast.
  • Tuesday: product deep dive with the Solutions team.
  • Wednesday: shadow two customer calls.
  • Thursday: support tooling training.
  • Friday: lunch with your onboarding buddy.

Do not worry about remembering everything. The week is designed so that you hear a lot and own very little. That is intentional.

Your first 90 days

By day 30, your job is to learn the product. Use it the way customers do, read the release notes, and sit in on as many calls as you can. Ask your buddy anything.

By day 60, you will own five accounts alongside your buddy. You will write the account plans, handle the day-to-day questions, and bring the hard ones to your buddy before they become urgent.

By day 90, you will run quarterly business reviews on your own. Your buddy will still be a phone call away, but the customer should feel that they are talking to you.
