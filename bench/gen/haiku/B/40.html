Staff password and multi-factor authentication FAQ

The new staff password and multi-factor authentication policy takes effect on 2026-11-03. This page answers the questions we hear most often.

Be careful about scams IT will never ask for your password or your code by email or phone. If someone does, do not reply. Report it to the help desk.

Why are we changing the policy?

Two phishing incidents in 2026 compromised staff mailboxes. The new policy makes accounts harder to take over.

What changes on November 3?

  • Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
  • Every staff account needs multi-factor authentication.

Do I have to change my password every 90 days?

No. We are removing forced rotation. We will only ask you to change your password after a suspected compromise.

What if I do not have a smartphone?

The help desk will issue you a hardware key at no cost.

What if I lose my phone or key?

Call the help desk at extension 4400. They will give you a temporary bypass code, valid for 24 hours.

Who do I contact for help?

The help desk is open from 07:00 to 17:00 on school days.
