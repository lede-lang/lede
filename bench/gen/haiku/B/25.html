Database for the member-profile service

Accounts team, Lattice Fitness. Decision due 2026-11-04.

Workload

The service stores 9 million member profiles of about 2 KB each. At peak it handles 3,500 reads and 220 writes per second. Most reads are key lookups. A few admin searches look up members by email or by gym.

Options

  • PostgreSQL (RDS)
  • DynamoDB
  • MongoDB Atlas

Pros and cons

PostgreSQL (RDS)

  • Pro: flexible queries, which the admin search needs.
  • Pro: familiar to the team.
  • Con: scaling is vertical plus read replicas, and multi-region is replicas only.

DynamoDB

  • Pro: scales automatically, with low ops effort.
  • Pro: global tables for multi-region.
  • Con: key lookups only, so admin searches need extra work.

MongoDB Atlas

  • Pro: flexible queries and global clusters.
  • Con: the highest cost today, and sharding adds operational work.

Recommendation

Recommended: PostgreSQL Admin search needs flexible queries. At our realistic 3x horizon, PostgreSQL costs within $220 a month of DynamoDB ($1,640 against $1,420).
Warning If write volume rises above 2,000 per second, we would have to revisit sharding for PostgreSQL.
