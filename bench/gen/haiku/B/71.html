Telemetry pipeline architecture

Skerry Wind Power · Owner: Asset Data team · Region: eu-north-1 · Reviewed 2026-10-02

Overview

Turbine readings from each of the six offshore wind farms flow into one central stream, and they are processed once there. The processed data is then split into a fast store for dashboards and a cheap archive for audits.

If the uplink fails

The Edge Gateway buffers up to 6 hours of readings on disk. When the link returns, it replays them in order.

Outages longer than 6 hours lose data. After that limit, the oldest readings are dropped. Do not treat history older than the buffer as complete.
