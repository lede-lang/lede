Heat-Wave Demand Emergency Runbook

For the on-duty system operator · Version 2026-10-10 · Available supply 5,200 MW

When to use

Use this runbook when the day-ahead forecast or live demand reaches 92 percent of available supply.

A stage is declared by the Duty Director, never by a single operator. If you believe a stage is needed, call the Duty Director and wait for the declaration before acting on it.

Stages

Stage 1 · 92% of supply

4,784 MW
  • Issue a conservation alert by text and app to 380,000 customers.
  • Ask large industrial customers on interruptible contracts to get ready to reduce load on 30 minutes of notice.
  • Confirm that standby generation at 3 peaking plants can start within 20 minutes.

Stage 2 · 97% of supply

5,044 MW
  • Call the demand response program, which delivers about 210 MW.
  • Start buying emergency imports from the neighboring utility.
  • Brief the regulator by phone.

Stage 3 · 100% of supply

5,200 MW
  • Begin rotating outages in blocks A to L, in order.
  • Each block is off for at most 60 minutes.
  • Never repeat a block before all twelve have been used.

Steps for rotating outages

Hospitals, the 911 center and water pumping stations must never be switched off. Check the exclusion list before every block is opened.
  1. Get approval
    1. The Duty Director approves in the log.
    2. The regulator is told within 30 minutes.
  2. Protect critical sites
    1. Confirm that hospitals, the 911 center and water pumping stations are excluded.
    2. Check that the exclusion list was updated this month.
  3. Run the blocks
    1. Open the first block's feeders.
    2. Restore it after 60 minutes at most, then open the next block.
    3. Update the public outage map every 15 minutes.
  4. Stand down
    1. End rotating outages when demand stays below 95 percent for 60 minutes.
    2. Restore all blocks.
    3. File the event report within 24 hours.
