Triage flow

Steps

  1. Check the consumer pods in the streaming namespace.
  2. Check per-partition lag.
  3. If one partition holds more than 40 percent of the lag, treat it as skew.
  4. Scale the deployment to at most 24 replicas, because the topic has 24 partitions.
Never reset consumer offsets to latest Unprocessed settlements would be skipped, and they would have to be reconciled by hand.
Escalation If lag is still growing after 30 minutes, escalate to the Streaming lead.
