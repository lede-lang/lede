How to read your water bill

Your bill comes every three months. It can look busy at first, so this page walks through each line, with a worked example you can check against your own.

Your bill at a glance

Every bill has three parts: the charge for the water you used, a sewer charge, and a fixed service charge. The example below is for a household that used 72 cubic meters (m3) in the quarter.

Total for the quarter in this example: $362.94.

Usage charges

Water is metered in cubic meters (m3) and charged in three tiers. The more you use, the higher the rate for the extra water.

TierAmount usedRate per m3
First tierThe first 30 m3$1.85
Second tierThe next 30 m3$2.60
Third tierAnything above 60 m3$3.90

In the example, 72 m3 breaks down like this:

Part of the usageCalculationAmount
First 30 m330 m3 at $1.85$55.50
Next 30 m330 m3 at $2.60$78.00
Above 60 m312 m3 at $3.90$46.80
Usage charge$55.50 + $78.00 + $46.80$180.30

Fixed charges

Sewer charge: 80 percent of your usage charge. In the example, 80 percent of $180.30 is $144.24.

Service charge: a fixed $38.40 per quarter, whatever you use.

Add the usage charge, the sewer charge and the service charge together to get your total. In the example: $180.30 + $144.24 + $38.40 = $362.94.

Ways to pay

  • Online at tarnvalleywater.example/pay
  • By phone on 555-0121
  • In person at the Main Street office, on weekdays from 8:30 to 16:30

Payment is due 21 days after the issue date. If you sign up for autopay, we take $2 off each bill.

If you cannot pay on time

Please call us before the due date on 555-0133, and we will help you set up a payment plan. If a bill is not paid, a late fee of 5 percent is added after 30 days. Calling early is the best way to avoid it.
