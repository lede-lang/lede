Households served were 6,840 in July, 7,310 in August and 7,925 in September. That is 16.2, 16.0 and 16.3 pounds per household.

  • September distribution was up 16.6 percent on July, while households served grew 15.9 percent. The amount per household held steady.
  • Produce grew fastest, up 26.9 percent from July to September. The new partnership with three local farms started on 15 August 2026.
  • Shelf-stable dipped in August, because of a delayed shipment from the regional network.
