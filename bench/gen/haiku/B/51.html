Summary

A storage policy change was meant to restrict bucket access to our VPC endpoint. It also blocked uploads made with pre-signed URLs from agents' phones. Every mobile upload returned 403 until the deny statement was removed.

Timeline

All times UTC.

  • 10:15Policy change applied through Terraform. Uploads are not yet failing.
  • 10:22Uploads begin failing with 403, seven minutes after the change.
  • 10:31First customer ticket reaches support.
  • 10:48Incident declared Sev2.
  • 11:20The policy diff is identified as the cause.
  • 11:58Hotfix applied, removing the deny statement.
  • 12:07Uploads recover.

Why it was not caught

  • The change was tested only from the office network.
  • Staging has no mobile upload test.
  • The policy linter does not model pre-signed URLs.

What went well

  • The declared incident had a clear commander within 17 minutes.
  • The rollback itself took under 10 minutes once the cause was known.
