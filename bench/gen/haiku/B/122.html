Drift Harbor: Rolling Back a Bad Client Patch

Game: Drift Harbor · Owner: Live Ops · Version 2026-10-13

When to roll back

After a patch release, roll back when either condition is met:

  • The crash rate is above 2 percent of sessions for 15 minutes.
  • Login failures are above 5 percent for 10 minutes.

The Live Ops lead must approve the rollback in the #liveops-war-room channel.

Do not roll back if

The patch contained a database schema migration, such as migration 0087 in patch 4.13. Rolling back after a schema migration corrupts player inventories. Older clients cannot read the new schema, so go forward with a hotfix instead.

Rollback steps

1

Stop the bleeding

  1. Pause the deploy pipeline.
  2. Set matchmaking to maintenance mode with the in-game message “Back soon”.
2

Point players to the last good build

  1. In the release console, set the live manifest to build 4.12.3.
  2. Invalidate the CDN cache, which takes about 10 minutes.
3

Verify

  1. Install the build on 3 test accounts, one each on PC, console and mobile.
  2. Confirm login and a full match on each.
4

Reopen in stages

  1. Let in 10 percent of players.
  2. After 5 minutes, open to 25 percent.
  3. After 5 more minutes, open to 50 percent.
  4. After another 5 minutes, open to 100 percent if the crash rate stays under 1 percent.
5

Communicate

  1. Post a status update within 30 minutes of the decision.
  2. Schedule compensation of 500 gems for every active account.
