Overview

Three accounts are joined by a transit gateway. All internet-bound traffic is inspected before it leaves. A Direct Connect link runs back to our Columbus data center.

Routing rules

Staging must never route to prod. Auditors ask about this rule specifically. Staging may never route to prod, in either direction through the transit gateway.
  • No internet gateways in workload VPCs.
  • The Direct Connect link reaches prod only.
