Omaha could take up to 7,200 more parcels a day, but rerouting there adds a day of transit.

Memphis and Tulsa plan

Reroute 2,200 Memphis parcels a day to Tulsa from 2026-11-23.

Memphis then runs at exactly 100.0 percent of capacity (55,000 a day). Tulsa rises to 99.5 percent (41,800 of 42,000). Both depots would be at their limit with no slack.

Risks

  • An outage or weather delay at Memphis or Tulsa during the window has nowhere to go except Omaha, and rerouting there adds a day of transit.
  • The plan relies on all 640 seasonal workers starting by 2026-11-16, including the 210 for Memphis.
