Summary

A weekly workout reminder campaign was meant to go out once to 1,240,000 users. A retry bug in the queue consumer re-sent each message six extra times. Users therefore got the same notification seven times, 7,440,000 duplicates in total, over 38 minutes.

Timeline

All times Pacific.

  • 16:00Campaign released.
  • 16:04The first consumer timeouts begin and retries start.
  • 16:09Social media complaints appear.
  • 16:15Support pages the Messaging on-call.
  • 16:22On-call pauses the campaign sender.
  • 16:38Queue purged and duplicates stop.

Contributing factors

The consumer treated a slow downstream acknowledgement as a failure, and it had no idempotency key, so a retry could not be recognized as a repeat. The campaign tool allows a send to the entire user base without a staged ramp. The pause switch took 7 minutes to find because it was not documented.

Customer impact

Users received the same workout reminder up to seven times. Opt-outs from push jumped to 3.4 percent of recipients, 42,160 users, against a normal rate of 0.2 percent. The app store rating dropped from 4.6 to 4.3 over the following week.
