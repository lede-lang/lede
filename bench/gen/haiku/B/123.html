Walk-in Refrigerator Alarm or Temperature Above 41°F

Owner: Operations Manager · Effective 2026-11-01 · Read on your phone in the warehouse

First two minutes

  • Silence the alarm.
  • Check the door is fully closed.
  • Read the display and the backup thermometer inside.
  • Write the time on the log sheet.

Decide what to do

Above 41 and up to 45°F, for less than 2 hours:

  • Move dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals into the backup chest freezers right away.
  • Keep produce in place.
  • Call the refrigeration technician.

Above 45°F, or above 41°F for 2 hours or more, or if nobody knows how long:

Dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals must be thrown away, with a second volunteer present to confirm. Produce, canned and packaged goods can stay.

Never taste or sniff food to decide whether it is safe.

Steps in order

  1. Call ChillRight, the refrigeration vendor, on 555-0134 (open 24 hours). Ask for a 4-hour response.
  2. Message the Operations Manager.
  3. Move or discard items as above.
  4. Record each item thrown away on the waste log with its weight in pounds.
  5. After the repair, run the refrigerator for 4 hours below 38°F before restocking.
