Welcome to Cedarwood Open House

A letter and a guide for families, from principal Dr. Leonie Osei.

Dear families,

We would love to meet you. Our open house is a chance to walk through the school, see the classrooms, and ask us anything. Bring your questions and your children, and take your time. We are glad you are here.

When and where

Thursday, November 12, 2026

5:30 to 7:30 in the evening

Main building, 410 Elm Street

Schedule

  • 17:30 Welcome from the principal, in the gym.
  • 17:50 Classroom tours in groups of 8 families. Each tour takes about 30 minutes.
  • 18:30 Kindergarten information session, in the library.
  • 18:45 After-school program table, in the cafeteria.
  • 19:15 Closing words.

What to bring

If you plan to register your child for kindergarten in the 2027-28 school year, please bring:

  • A birth certificate
  • Proof of address
  • Immunization records

Registration opens on January 12, 2027.

Getting here

  • Free parking is available in the Elm Street lot, which has 60 spaces.
  • A free shuttle runs every 10 minutes from the Pine Street church lot.
  • Childcare for siblings is offered in the music room.

Questions

Please call us on 555-0166, or write to office@cedarwood.example. We are always happy to hear from you.

With warmth,
Dr. Leonie Osei
Principal, Cedarwood Elementary
