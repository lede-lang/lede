Prescription refill flow

Medora Health · Integrations team · Version dated 2026-09-30

Controlled substances are never refilled through the app.

Refill sequence

Four parties: Patient App, Refill Service, Clinic EHR, Pharmacy Portal.

  1. 1
    Patient App → Refill Service
    request refill
  2. 2
    Refill Service → Clinic EHR
    check active prescription and refills left
  3. 3
    Clinic EHR → Refill Service
    approved, 2 refills remaining
  4. 4
    Refill Service → Pharmacy Portal
    e-prescription, NCPDP SCRIPT
  5. 5
    Pharmacy Portal → Refill Service
    accepted, ready in 2 hours
  6. 6
    Refill Service → Patient App
    refill confirmed

Alternative: the clinic declines

  1. 3
    Clinic EHR → Refill Service
    denied, visit needed
  2. 6
    Refill Service → Patient App
    book a visit

Service targets

CallTarget
Refill Service to Clinic EHR (prescription check)Within 3 s
Refill Service to Pharmacy Portal (acknowledgement)Within 30 s
Confirmation shown in Patient App, from the requestWithin 10 s

Rules

  • Controlled substances are never refilled through the app.
  • A refill covers at most a 90-day supply.
  • Requests made after 21:00 wait in a queue until 07:00.
