Annual report, 2023 to 2025

Greenhouse gas emissions, 2023 to 2025

Total emissions fell 13.7 percent between 2023 and 2025, to 22,870 tonnes CO2e. Most of what remains is scope 3, and that is the hard part.

Scope 1 and scope 2 are both shrinking. Scope 3 is shrinking more slowly, and it is now most of the total. Reaching the 2030 target means taking 4,320 tonnes off the 2025 level.

Method and caveats

  • Scope 3 covers purchased materials, upstream transport and business travel only. It does not include the use of sold products.
  • The 2023 baseline was restated in 2025, after supplier data improved.
  • The inventory follows the GHG Protocol. It has not yet been externally assured.
