Other paths

  • Submission not complete: Return to applicant.
  • Project value 50000 dollars or less: Fast-track review, then Permit issued, labeled approved.
  • Project value over 50000 dollars, not in a historic district: Plan review directly, then Permit issued, labeled approved.

Review times

StageTarget
Completeness check2 business days
Fast-track review3 business days
Plan review15 business days
Heritage Board review30 days, added on top of the plan review time

Fees

Every application pays a base fee of 180 dollars. Projects valued above 50000 dollars also pay 0.8 percent of the project value above 50000 dollars.

Example: a 250000-dollar project

180 base fee + 0.8% of 200000 (= 1600) = 1780 dollars
