Digital press: lease or buy

Orchard Press · Decision owner: Lucinda Marsh · For the meeting on 2026-11-12

Orchard Press needs a digital press for its commercial print shop. The press prints about 2.4 million pages a month. We compare buying and leasing over a four-year horizon.

Options

Buy. Purchase price of 412000 dollars. A service contract costs 14400 dollars a year, or 57600 over four years. We expect to resell the press for 120000 dollars after four years.

Lease. 7650 dollars a month for 48 months, which is 367200 dollars. Service is included. A deposit of two monthly payments (15300 dollars) is due up front. At the end there is an optional buyout for 95000 dollars.

Risks

Buying ties up cash that we need for the new bindery. The resale value of 120000 dollars is a guess, so the true cost could be higher or lower.

Leasing locks us in for 48 months. The buyout price of 95000 dollars is fixed today, which limits the surprise at the end.

Recommendation

We recommend the lease.

Leasing costs 17600 dollars more than buying over four years (367,200 against 349,600). We accept that cost because the lease needs no large cash outlay, which leaves the money for the new bindery.

Per page printed, over 115.2 million pages in four years, the buy option costs about 0.30 cents and the lease about 0.32 cents.
