Dispatch queue backlog

On-call runbook · Quickpost Couriers Dispatch · Owner: Dispatch Engineering

Never purge dispatch-jobs.Every message in this queue is a real customer delivery. Purging loses deliveries.

Symptoms

QUEUE_DEPTH_HIGH fires when queue depth stays above 5,000 for 10 minutes. Normal depth is under 400.
  • Drivers see new jobs arrive late in the app.

Diagnose

  • Check the queue depth and consumer count: 
    kubectl -n dispatch get hpa dispatch-consumer
  • Look at the oldest message age on the queue dashboard.
  • Check the database connection pool. It is capped at 200 connections.

Fix steps

  1. Scale the consumers.
    1. Run: 
      kubectl -n dispatch scale deploy dispatch-consumer --replicas=24
    2. Wait 5 minutes and re-check.
    3. If depth is still rising, scale to 40 replicas. That is the maximum the database pool supports.
  2. Check for poison messages.
    1. Look for a message that has been retried more than 10 times.
    2. Move it to dispatch-dlq with the dlq-move tool.
  3. Pause the bulk import scheduler in the admin console. Bulk imports add up to 3,000 jobs at a time.
  4. Re-enable the scheduler gradually once depth drops under 400.

Escalation

If depth is still above 5,000 after 20 minutes, page the Dispatch Engineering lead and open an incident in #incidents.
