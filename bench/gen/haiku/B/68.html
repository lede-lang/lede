Volunteer welcome

Welcome to the Bayside Community Pantry

We are so glad you are here. Thank you for giving your time to your neighbors.

Hours

Tuesday9:00 to 12:00
Thursday16:00 to 19:00
Saturday8:00 to 12:00

You can come to as many shifts as you like.

New volunteers join a 45-minute orientation at 8:00 on the first Saturday of each month, before their first shift. The next one is on Saturday 7 November 2026.

Your first shift

  • Arrive 15 minutes early.
  • Check in with the shift lead. They wear a green apron.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes.
  • Leave your valuables in the locker.
  • Nobody lifts more than 25 pounds on their own. Ask for help.

Roles

You may be asked to do any of these:

  • Sorting donations
  • Stocking shelves
  • Packing family boxes
  • Greeting clients at the door
  • Helping carry boxes to cars

Clients are neighbors. We do not ask about their income, and we do not take photos.

Getting here

Parking is behind the building on Harbor Street. The bus stop for route 14 is across the road.

If you cannot make a shift, please tell us at least 24 hours ahead by texting 555-0142, so we can find cover.

Thank you

Last year, 312 volunteers gave 9,400 hours. Together you helped us serve 41,000 meals a month on average. We could not do it without you. Thank you.
