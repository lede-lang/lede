Takeaways

  1. Onboarding changes shipped on 20 July 2026 line up with the step from 48 percent at month 1 (July cohort) to 51 percent (August cohort).
  2. The biggest drop is always between month 0 and month 1. Every cohort loses between 47 and 54 points there.
  3. September's cohort is the largest, at 29,800 signups, and it is also the best retaining so far, at 53 percent at month 1.
