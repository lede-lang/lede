Our 2026 engagement survey: what you told us

Thank you to everyone who answered. Here is what the results say, what is better than last year, and what we will do next.

Response

842people responded, out of 1,060 employees

Change since 2025

Change in favorable score, in percentage points. Two statements rose by 3 points: manager feedback and recommending the company. Growth fell by 3 points, and it is also our lowest score.

What we will do

  1. Publish the career framework by February 28, 2027, so everyone can see what growth here looks like.
  2. Train 90 managers on giving feedback, starting December 1, 2026.
  3. Run a short pulse check in April 2027, to see whether the changes are working.

Thank you for the honest answers. The growth result is the one we will work on first.
