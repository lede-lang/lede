911 Call Receipt and Dispatch Architecture

Owner: Public Safety Systems team · Reviewed 2026-09-30

Overview

Calls arrive from the telephone carriers into the primary dispatch center. A backup dispatch center across the county can take over if the primary is lost.

The backup center is never activated automatically. It is activated only when the shift supervisor declares the primary dispatch center unavailable. Until that declaration, the standby router and replica keep running, but no one moves calls or staff to the backup center.
