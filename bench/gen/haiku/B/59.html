Architecture council, decision 4 November 2026

Login: build on Keycloak, or buy Authly or Gatepost

Meadowbrook has about 250,000 monthly active learners, growing roughly 40 percent a year. Our home-grown login system is a maintenance burden, and the platform lead asks the council to choose its replacement.

Risks

  • Keycloak. We own bot protection and the upkeep of a self-hosted system. The cost rises to $330,000 a year at 500,000 users, and it depends on 1.5 engineers.
  • Authly. The cost rises to $288,000 a year at 500,000 users, approaching the self-hosted figure. We would need to revisit the choice at that size.
  • Gatepost. It is the cheapest option at every size we modelled. Its price rises sharply above 400,000 users, bot protection is extra, and EU residency is only on the highest plan. Even with bot protection added, Gatepost at 250,000 users would cost $126,000 a year, below Authly's $151,000. The case for Authly rests on what it includes without add-ons.

Recommendation

Buy Authly. It is the middle option on cost at 250,000 users, at $151,000 a year, and it is the only one that covers bot protection, adaptive MFA and EU residency without add-ons.

Gatepost is cheaper, and a pure price comparison would favour it. We recommend Authly for what it includes. We would revisit this decision at 500,000 monthly users, when Authly's annual cost reaches $288,000.

Decision date: 4 November 2026.
