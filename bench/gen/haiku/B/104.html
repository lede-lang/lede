Tallgrass Telemetry Pipeline

Owner: Asset Performance · Reviewed 2026-10-06 · Audience: Control Room team

Overview

Turbine data is collected on site and summarized before it is sent over a limited link. The grid operator receives output and availability figures directly from the site.

Pipeline

Sizing

ItemFigure
Signals62 turbines with 140 signals each
Samples per second8,680
Samples per dayabout 750 million
Full-resolution retention30 days
One-minute average retention5 years
Fiber link100 Mbps, full resolution
Satellite fallback2 Mbps, one-minute averages only
Edge Historian buffer72 hours of full-resolution data

On satellite fallback, the Control Room sees averages, not full-resolution data. The 2 Mbps link carries one-minute averages only.

The Edge Historian can hold 72 hours of full-resolution data until the fiber link returns. Full-resolution data is not lost during an outage of that length; it is sent once the fiber link is back.
