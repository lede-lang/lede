Disaster recovery drill: patient scheduling

Platform Resilience, Bramble Health. Drill run on 2026-10-07. Runbook and results.

Scope

This drill restored the patient-scheduling service in us-east-2 from backups, after simulating the complete loss of us-west-2. Nothing in us-west-2 was used during the restore. The goal was to measure recovery time and data loss against our targets and to confirm that the runbook below works as written.

Restore procedure

  1. Freeze writes in us-west-2.
  2. Restore the latest RDS snapshot in us-east-2.
  3. Restore the S3 attachments bucket from the replica.
  4. Deploy the service stack from the release tag.
Warning before the DNS switch Confirm the snapshot timestamp before you continue. Promoting an old snapshot silently loses appointments booked after that point. Compare the timestamp with the last confirmed write in us-west-2 and stop if the gap is larger than the RPO target.
  1. Switch DNS for scheduling.bramble.example to us-east-2.
  2. Run the smoke test suite.
  3. Reopen writes.
