Route 9 Transit Options

Riverbend City Council decision page · Council vote 2026-12-02 · Transportation Department

Three ways to improve the Route 9 transit corridor. The department recommends bus rapid transit, and needs the council to approve an option on 2026-12-02 to meet the federal grant deadline.

Trade-offs

OptionWhat you getWhat it costs you
Enhanced busCheapest and fastest to deliver.Carries the fewest riders, and still shares congested lanes.
Bus rapid transitThe middle path. Could be upgraded to rail later.Mid-range cost and a 4-year build.
Light railCarries the most riders and tends to attract development.Takes 8 years, and the city could not fund its share without a bond measure.

Recommendation

The department recommends bus rapid transit.

The council needs to approve bus rapid transit on 2026-12-02. The federal grant can cover up to 50 percent of capital cost, but the application is due 2027-01-31 and requires a council-approved option. Without the vote on 2026-12-02, the city cannot meet the grant deadline.
