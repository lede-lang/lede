Your home-office stipend, explained

The home-office stipend takes effect on January 1, 2027. This page covers who gets it, what it pays for, and how to claim it, so you can find the answers without opening a ticket.

Who is covered

All employees who work at least 3 days a week from home are covered, once they have completed 90 days of service. Part-time employees receive 50 percent of the amounts described below.

What you get

$500one-time setup stipend
$40 a monthtoward internet and phone

The setup stipend can be used again after 3 years.

What you can buy

  • A desk
  • A chair
  • A monitor
  • A keyboard
  • A headset
  • Lighting

How to claim

  • Submit your receipts in the expense portal within 60 days of purchase.
  • Purchases over $250 need written approval from your manager before you buy.
  • Reimbursement arrives within 10 business days.

What is not covered

  • Gaming equipment
  • Furniture for non-work use
  • Software subscriptions
  • Anything the company already supplies to you
