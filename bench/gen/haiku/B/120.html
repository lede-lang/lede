How to read your electricity bill

Eastgrove Power, Customer Education. Rates effective 1 November 2026.

Your bill at a glance

Your bill has three main parts: a fixed monthly service charge, a delivery charge for every kilowatt-hour (kWh) you use, and a supply charge that depends on when you use power.

Sales tax of 5.2 percent applies to the total. The bill is due 21 days after the bill date.

What each charge means

ChargeWhat it coversRate
Service chargeA fixed amount each month, whatever you use$14.50 a month
Delivery chargeMoving power to your home, for every kWh$0.045 per kWh
Supply charge, peakThe power itself, 16:00 to 21:00 on weekdays$0.31 per kWh
Supply charge, off-peakThe power itself, at most other times$0.12 per kWh
Supply charge, super off-peakThe power itself, 00:00 to 06:00 every day$0.08 per kWh

Peak power costs more than 2.5 times as much as off-peak power. Using power at the right time can make a real difference.

Late payments: a fee of 1.5 percent is added to any unpaid balance after the due date.

Time-of-use hours

Here is how a weekday is divided up. Weekends have no peak period, so all their hours are off-peak, except that 00:00 to 06:00 is always super off-peak.

Super off-peak, $0.08 per kWh Off-peak, $0.12 per kWh Peak, $0.31 per kWh

Ways to lower your bill

  • Run the dishwasher and the laundry after 21:00, when power is off-peak.
  • Precool your house before 16:00. During peak hours, set the thermostat 2 degrees higher.
  • If you have an electric vehicle, charge it after midnight.

Lower your bill for less: budget billing spreads your year into 12 equal monthly payments, reviewed every 6 months. Income-qualified households can get a 20 percent discount through the CARE program. Enrolling takes about 10 minutes online.
