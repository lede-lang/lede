Ticketing platform architecture

Tidewater Ferries · Owner: Digital Channels · Region: North Europe · Reviewed 2026-09-22

Overview

Passengers book through Azure Front Door. Every booking is written to one database and announced as an event, so that the scanners at the terminals stay in step.
