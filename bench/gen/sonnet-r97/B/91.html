When to rotate

Rotate when a certificate has 30 days or fewer left. The CERT_EXPIRY_30D alert announces it.

Trigger30 days or fewerleft on a certificate
AlertCERT_EXPIRY_30Dannounces it
Coverage12 hostnamescovered by the certificates

Before you start

Check 1Have the change record open.
Check 2Confirm no deploy is running in #deploys.
Check 3Check that the internal CA is healthy.

Rotation steps

Never delete the old private key before step 6 Keep the old private key in place until the old certificate has been revoked in step 6. Rollback depends on it.

  1. Confirm expiry

    1. Run openssl x509 -enddate -noout on the live certificate.
    2. Compare the date with the alert.

  2. Request the new certificate

    1. Run certctl request --profile edge-prod.
    2. Check that the output lists all 12 hostnames.

  3. Stage it on the canary proxy edge-canary-01 only canary

    1. Copy it to /etc/edge/certs/next.
    2. Reload the proxy.

  4. Verify the canary 20 minutes

    1. openssl s_client -connect edge-canary-01:443 shows the new expiry date.
    2. Handshake errors stay under 0.1 percent for 20 minutes.

  5. Roll out to the other 17 proxies batches of 3

    Go in batches of 3, waiting 5 minutes between batches.

  6. Revoke the old certificate after 48 hours 48 hours

    Record the revocation in the change record. Only now is it safe to remove the old private key.

Rollback

The previous certificate stays in /etc/edge/certs/previous for 7 days.

Window7 days
Location/etc/edge/certs/previous

  1. Copy it back

    Copy the previous certificate back into place.

  2. Reload

    Reload the proxy.
