## Steps

1. Request the new certificate in the certificate manager.
2. Validate it via the DNS record.
3. Wait for the status **Issued**.

> **Warning: never remove the old certificate first.**
>
> Clients will get TLS handshake errors until the new one is attached. Attach the new certificate, then remove the old one later (step 6).

4. Attach the new certificate to the listener on **`edge-alb-prod`**.
5. Test with an external checker.
6. Remove the old certificate after **24 hours**.

> **Note:** this is manual for now because the automated renewal job is broken until ticket **EDGE-812** is fixed.
