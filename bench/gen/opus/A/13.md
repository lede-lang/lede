```mermaid
    lint -- "pass" --> build
    build --> scan
    scan -- "no critical CVEs" --> staging
    staging --> smoke
    smoke -- "pass" --> approval
    approval -- "approved" --> canary
    canary -- "healthy 30 min" --> full
    canary -- "error rate above 1%" --> rollback
```

## Rules

- **Manual Approval** is required only for services tagged **tier-1**.
- **Friday deploys** after 15:00 local time are blocked.
- **Auto Rollback** pages the owning team.
