# Data Reliability Team Charter

**Osprey Freight · Effective 2026-11-01 · Led by Grace Mbeki, with 5 engineers**

## Mission

Make every dataset the business relies on trustworthy, on time and explained.

## What we own

- The freshness and quality checks on the 40 tier-1 tables
- The data incident process
- The lineage catalog

## What we do not own

- **Dashboards and their business logic.** Analytics owns them.
- **Source application databases.** The product teams that run them own them.
- **Ad-hoc data requests.**

## How we work

- **Two-week sprints.**
- **A rotating data on-call**, with a 30-minute response time for tier-1 incidents during business hours.
- **A written review for every incident**, completed within 5 business days.

## How to reach us

- Post in the **#data-reliability** channel, or
- Open a ticket in the **DREL** queue.

> **Note:** Requests sent by direct message will be redirected to the DREL queue.
