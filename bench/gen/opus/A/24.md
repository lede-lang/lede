# Frontend Framework for the Course-Scheduling Admin App

**Wrenfield University IT · Internal Tools guild**

The admin app has about 60 screens, mostly forms and tables, and 5 developers will rewrite it. We compared React, Vue and Svelte.

> **Recommendation: React.** Four of our five developers already know it, and the data grid we need exists today.

## Criteria

- Team familiarity
- Form and table libraries
- Hiring pool
- Bundle size
- Long-term support

## Comparison

| Framework | Team familiarity | Form/table ecosystem | Bundle size (KB) | Hiring pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| React | 4 of 5 developers | Excellent | 142 | Large |
| Vue | 1 of 5 developers | Good | 98 | Medium |
| Svelte | 0 of 5 developers | Fair | 41 | Small |

## Pros and cons

### React

**Pros**
- Most of the team already knows it
- Mature form and data-grid libraries
- Largest hiring pool

**Cons**
- Largest bundle, at 142 KB
- More choices to make about state and forms

### Vue

**Pros**
- Smaller bundle than React
- Good form and table ecosystem

**Cons**
- Only one developer knows it
- Medium hiring pool

### Svelte

**Pros**
- Smallest bundle by far, at 41 KB
- The team's favorite in the prototype spike

**Cons**
- No one on the team has shipped with it
- Thinner form and table ecosystem
- Small hiring pool

## Recommendation

> **Choose React.** Four of the five developers already know it, so the rewrite starts without a learning curve. The data grid our table-heavy screens need is available today rather than something we would have to build. Its larger bundle matters little for an internal admin app.

> "We are optimizing for the next five years of maintenance, not the first five weeks of building."
>
> — Hannah Osei, guild lead

---

**Note:** Svelte was the favorite in the prototype spike. We will revisit it for smaller tools.
