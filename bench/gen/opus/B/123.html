Walk-in fridge alarm or temperature above 41°F

  • Owner: Operations Manager
  • Effective: 2026-11-01
Use this when the walk-in refrigerator alarm sounds or the display reads above 41°F.
Call ChillRight 555-0134 · open 24 hours
Never taste or sniff food to decide if it is safe

Decide by temperature and time only, using the rules below.

First two minutes

  • Silence the alarm.
  • Check the door is fully closed.
  • Read the display and the backup thermometer inside.
  • Write the time on the log sheet.

Decide what to do

Move

41–45°F, and above 41°F for less than 2 hours

  • Move right away dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals into the backup chest freezers.
  • Keep produce in place.
  • Call the refrigeration technician.
Throw away

Above 45°F, or above 41°F for 2 hours or more, or nobody knows how long

  • Throw away dairy, eggs, meat and prepared meals, with a second volunteer present to confirm.
  • Keep produce, canned and packaged goods.

Steps in order

  1. Call ChillRight, the refrigeration vendor, on 555-0134 (open 24 hours) and ask for a 4-hour response.
  2. Message the Operations Manager.
  3. Move or discard items as above.
  4. Record each item thrown away on the waste log with its weight in pounds.
  5. After the repair, run the refrigerator for 4 hours below 38°F before restocking.
