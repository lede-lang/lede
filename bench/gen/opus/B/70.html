Windrow Software · Charter

Customer Advisory Council

Effective
January 1, 2027
Sponsor
Ines Carvalho, Chief Product Officer

Purpose

The Customer Advisory Council gives customers a structured, regular way to shape the Windrow product roadmap and to tell us plainly what is not working.

Membership

  • The council has 14 seats: 12 customer members and 2 Windrow product leads.
  • Members are chosen to reflect the customer base by company size and industry.
  • Customer members serve two-year terms, renewable once.
  • Half of the customer seats rotate each year, to keep continuity.
  • The chair is Ines Carvalho. The vice chair is elected by the customer members.

Meetings

The council meets quarterly, for two hours. Meetings in 2027:

  • Q1March 10
  • Q2June 9
  • Q3September 8
  • Q4December 8
  • The agenda is sent 10 days before each meeting.
  • Written minutes are shared within 5 business days after each meeting.
  • A quorum is 8 members, including at least 6 customer members.

What the council does and does not do

The council does

  • Review roadmap themes.
  • Test early designs.
  • Rank customer-submitted requests.

The council does not

  • Approve pricing.
  • Commit Windrow to a delivery date.
  • Replace the normal support channel.

Confidentiality

  • All members sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before the first meeting.
  • Roadmap material shown to the council is confidential unless marked otherwise.

Sponsored by Ines Carvalho, Chief Product Officer, Windrow Software. Effective January 1, 2027.
