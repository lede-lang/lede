Takeaways

  1. The onboarding changes shipped on July 20, 2026 line up with the jump in month-1 retention from 48% (July cohort) to 51% (August cohort).
  2. The biggest drop is always between month 0 and month 1: every cohort loses roughly half its users in the first month.
  3. September's cohort is the largest so far (29,800 users) and also the best retaining, at 53% in month 1.
