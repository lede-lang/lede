Capital cost divided by expected weekday riders in 2035.

A federal grant can cover up to 50% of capital cost for whichever option is approved. The application is due 2027-01-31 and requires a council-approved option.

Trade-offs

Enhanced bus

  • Cheapest, and fastest to deliver (2 years)
  • Carries the fewest riders
  • Buses still share congested lanes

Bus rapid transit

  • The middle path on cost, time and riders
  • Could be upgraded to rail later
  • About twice enhanced bus's capital cost per rider

Light rail

  • Carries the most riders
  • Attracts development along the corridor
  • Takes 8 years to build
  • The city could not fund its share without a bond measure

Recommendation

Approve bus rapid transit

The transportation department recommends bus rapid transit: dedicated lanes and stations for $310M, built in 4 years, carrying an expected 26,000 weekday riders by 2035, with room to upgrade to rail later.

The council must approve it on 2026-12-02 for the city to meet the federal grant deadline.
