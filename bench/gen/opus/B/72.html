How a prescription refill reaches the pharmacy

The path of a refill request from the Patient App, through the clinic's record system, to the Pharmacy Portal.

  • ForIntegrations team
  • Version2026-09-30

Controlled substances are never refilled through the app.

This applies to every request, whatever the clinic or pharmacy.
