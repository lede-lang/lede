Overview

Three AWS accounts (Network, Prod and Staging) are joined by a transit gateway. All internet-bound traffic is inspected before it leaves, and a Direct Connect link runs back to our Columbus data center.

Three accounts, one hub

Prod and Staging VPCs attach to the Transit Gateway in the Network account.

Inspected egress

Internet-bound traffic passes the Network Firewall, then leaves via NAT.

Private link home

Direct Connect, 2 x 1 Gbps, to the Columbus Data Center.

Routing rules

  • !

    Staging may never route to prod AUDITORS ASK

    No route may exist from the Staging VPC (10.20.0.0/16) to the Claims VPC (10.10.0.0/16). This is the rule auditors check first.

  • 2

    No internet gateways in workload VPCs

    The Claims and Staging VPCs reach the internet only through the Transit Gateway, the Network Firewall and the NAT Gateway in the Inspection VPC.

  • 3

    Direct Connect reaches prod only

    Traffic from the Columbus Data Center over Direct Connect may reach the Prod account, not Staging.
