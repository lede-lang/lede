Pros and cons

PostgreSQL (RDS)

Pros

  • High query flexibility for admin search by email and gym
  • Within $220/month of DynamoDB at 3x traffic

Cons

  • Scales vertically plus read replicas
  • Multi-region via read replicas only

DynamoDB

Pros

  • Cheapest today and at 3x
  • Automatic scaling, low ops effort
  • Global tables

Cons

  • Low query flexibility for admin search
  • Costs more than PostgreSQL at 10x

MongoDB Atlas

Pros

  • Medium query flexibility
  • Global clusters

Cons

  • Most expensive at every traffic level
  • Medium ops effort with sharding

Recommendation

Recommendation

PostgreSQL (RDS). Admin search needs flexible queries, and its cost stays within $220 a month of DynamoDB at our realistic 3x horizon ($1,640 against $1,420).

Warning

Write volume above 2,000 per second would force us to revisit sharding.
