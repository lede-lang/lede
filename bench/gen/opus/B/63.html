Q3 2026: July to September

More food went out every month this quarter, to more households, and the amount each household received held steady. A message from our director, Camille Ferreira, and the team.

  • We kept pace with demand. Distribution grew 16.6% from July to September while households served grew 15.9%, so each household received about the same amount.
  • Produce grew fastest, up 26.9% from July to September, thanks to our new partnership with three local farms that began on August 15, 2026.
  • Shelf-stable food dipped in August because a shipment from the regional network was delayed. It recovered in September.
