Bayside Community Pantry

Welcome, new volunteer!

We're so glad you're here. This page has everything you need for your first shift.

From Rosa Delgado, Volunteer Coordinator

Hours

We are open for distribution three times a week:

  • Tuesday9:00 – 12:00
  • Thursday16:00 – 19:00
  • Saturday8:00 – 12:00

Come to as many shifts as you like.

Your first shift

Before your first shift, join our 45-minute orientation. It runs at 8:00 on the first Saturday of each month. The next one is on Saturday, November 7, 2026.

On the day:

  • Arrive 15 minutes early.
  • Check in with the shift lead. Look for the green apron.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes.
  • Leave your valuables in the locker.

Please lift safely. Nobody lifts more than 25 pounds on their own. Ask for help. We are happy to give it.

Roles

You may be asked to help with:

  • Sorting donations
  • Stocking shelves
  • Packing family boxes
  • Greeting clients at the door
  • Carrying boxes to cars
Our clients are our neighbors.
  • We do not ask about their income.
  • We do not take photos.

Getting here

By car: park behind the building on Harbor Street.

By bus: take route 14. The stop is across the road.

Can't make a shift? Please tell us at least 24 hours ahead. Text 555-0142 so we can find cover.
