Routing flow

Swipe sideways to see the whole flow, or read it in words below.

  1. Critical value: phone the ordering clinician. If read-back is confirmed within 15 minutes, log the read-back. If not, escalate to the department head, then log the read-back.
  2. Not critical: if flagged abnormal, add to the clinician inbox; otherwise file without alert. Either way, the result is then released to the patient portal.
