Handling steps

  1. Log the request within 1 business day

    Record it in the privacy tracker.

  2. Verify identity within 5 business days

    1. aAsk for two documents.
    2. bNever accept a photo of a card showing a full number.

  3. Confirm the scope

    Agree it with the requester by email.

  4. Collect the data

    From all six systems listed under Systems to search.

  5. Legal review

    Legal reviews the export and redacts other people's data.

  6. Package

    1. aEncrypted ZIP.
    2. bSend the password by SMS, never in the same email.

  7. Deliver by day 25

    Record the delivery date.

  8. Close the ticket

    Keep the log for 3 years.

Do not

  • Do not delete data in response to an access request.
  • Do not send the export to an address that was not verified.
  • Do not discuss the request in public channels.
