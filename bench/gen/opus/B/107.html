Net revenue range

Only the gala's whole range stays above the $100,000 goal. The walkathon reaches it only at the top of its range; the online campaign never reaches it on its own.

Risks

Spring gala: depends on a few large sponsorsIt also needs the most money up front ($60,000) and the most effort (about 400 hours).
Family walkathon: weather on the dayBad weather could pull it toward the bottom of its range, below the goal.
Online giving campaign: social media reachResults depend on how far the campaign spreads online. Even its best case falls short of the goal on its own.

Recommendation

Hold the spring gala, with the online campaign as an add-on

  • The gala is the only option whose whole range stays above the $100,000 goal.
  • The online campaign adds reach for only $8,000 and about 120 hours.

The board needs to approve the $60,000 gala budget on 2026-11-17.
