When the electronic health record is unavailable

  • For: Charge nurses and front-desk staff
  • Owner: Clinical Operations
  • Effective: 2026-11-15
The binder's allergy list is the source of truth

Until the record is back, use the allergy list in the downtime binder for every patient. Do not rely on memory or on older printouts.

Declare downtime

When
Record unavailable or unusably slow for 10 minutes
Announce
Charge nurse pages "Paper procedures" overhead
Call
IT service desk, extension 5000

During downtime

  1. Switch to paper

    1. Take the downtime binder from each station. It holds the printed schedule (refreshed at 05:00 daily), an allergy list and a medication list for every patient booked that day.
    2. Label every paper form with a patient label sheet.

  2. Check in patients

    1. Confirm identity with two identifiers: name and date of birth.
    2. Record the arrival time by hand.

  3. Orders and medications

    1. Write down every verbal order and read it back.
    2. Never give a high-risk medication without a second nurse's check.
    3. Record every dose on the paper medication sheet.

  4. Keep care moving

    1. Do not cancel visits solely because of downtime.
    2. Tell patients about any delay beyond 20 minutes.

Recovery

  1. When IT announces restorationWait for the all-clear before entering anything.
  2. Within 4 hoursScan paper documents into the record.
  3. Within 24 hoursReconcile medication entries.
  4. After the eventReport the event on the incident form.
