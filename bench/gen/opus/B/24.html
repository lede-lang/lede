  • Form and table libraries

  • Bundle size
  • Long-term support

Pros

Pros

  • Good ecosystem
  • Smaller bundle at 98 KB

Cons

Svelte

Pros

  • Smallest bundle at 41 KB
  • Favorite in the prototype spike

Cons

  • No one on the team knows it yet
  • Fair form/table ecosystem
  • Small hiring pool

Recommendation

Recommendation

React. Mainly because four of the five developers already know it, and the data grid we need exists today.

"We are optimizing for the next five years of maintenance, not the first five weeks of building."

Hannah Osei, guild lead

Note: Svelte was the favorite in the prototype spike, and we will revisit it for smaller tools.
