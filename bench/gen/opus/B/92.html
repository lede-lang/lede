Summary

The point-of-sale vendor added a column named tender_subtype to the sales feed without notice. The ingestion job failed on the unexpected column. The failure alert was routed to a retired channel, so nobody knew until a store manager asked why their report was empty.

Once the Data Platform on-call was paged, the cause was found, the schema mapping was patched and the reports were backfilled. No incorrect numbers were published.

Contributing factors

  • Alert routing. The failure alert still routed to the retired channel #data-alerts-old, so it reached no one.
  • No schema contract check. Vendor feeds had no check that would catch an unannounced column before ingestion.
  • Serial backfill. The backfill ran serially, which stretched recovery to 95 minutes.

What went well

  • Once paged, the on-call found the cause in 45 minutes (07:50 to 08:35).
  • No wrong numbers were published. Reports were late or empty, never incorrect.
