Lost or stolen laptop

  • Effective 2026-11-01
  • Owner IT Security
15minutes

First rule: report it within 15 minutes of noticing

Call the service desk as soon as you realise the laptop is missing.

Call extension 4100 Phone only. Do not report it by email.

Full-disk encryption is enforced on all laptops.A lost device is not automatically a data breach, but it must still be reported, every time.

Service desk steps

  1. Mark the device as lost in the device manager

    This locks it and starts a remote wipe the next time it connects.

  2. Disable the user's sessions and revoke their VPN certificate

  3. Check whether the laptop held client files outside the document management system

    This is not allowed, but it does happen. Ask the user and check before moving on.If it did, go straight to step 4.

  4. If it did, notify the General Counsel's office within 1 hour

    Client confidentiality rules may require telling the client.

  5. Order a replacement 1 business day

    Standard turnaround.

  6. File the report in the incident log within 24 hours
