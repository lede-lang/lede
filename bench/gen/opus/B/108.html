What the trade-off means

Every wrongly blocked transaction is a real customer declined at checkout. A higher catch rate stops more fraud, but if it also blocks more good payments, customers feel it directly.

Vantage Shield compared with Tollgate

+3.6 ptsmore fraud caught+1.2 ptsmore good transactions wrongly blocked+$194,400more in fees a year

Kestrel Risk compared with Tollgate

−1.7 ptsless fraud caught−0.7 ptsfewer good transactions wrongly blocked−$86,400less in fees a year

Recommendation

Lean toward Tollgate

Tollgate is the balanced choice: it catches 91.2% of fraud while wrongly blocking 1.9% of good transactions, at $295,200 a year.

Budget fallback: Kestrel Risk. If the steering group wants to cut fees by about $86,400 a year, Kestrel Risk costs $208,800 and blocks the fewest good transactions, but catches the least fraud (89.5%).
The change freeze rules out Vantage ShieldIf the contract is signed by 2026-12-15, Tollgate (6 weeks) and Kestrel Risk (4 weeks) would be live before the change freeze on 2027-02-01. Vantage Shield, at 10 weeks, would not.
