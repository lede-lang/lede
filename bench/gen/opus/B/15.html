Runbook · Database Reliability

Manual failover of orders-aurora-prod

Fenwick Bank · Aurora PostgreSQL cluster

When to use this runbook

Use this runbook in either of these cases:

  • The writer instance has been unhealthy for more than 5 minutes and automatic failover has not happened.
  • A planned failover during the Sunday maintenance window.
Sunday maintenance window: 02:00 to 04:00 UTC

Prerequisites

  • The break-glass role db-failover-operator
  • Access to the #db-incidents channel
  • The replica lag dashboard open

Failover steps

  1. Announce the failover in #db-incidents.
  2. Confirm replica lag on the target reader is under 30 seconds.
  3. Put the orders service into read-only mode with the feature flag orders.readonly.
  4. Stop and check before this step
    Never fail over to a reader whose lag exceeds 30 seconds. Committed orders will be lost.
    Trigger the failover to reader instance orders-aurora-prod-r2.
  5. Wait for the cluster endpoint to point at the new writer (usually 30 to 60 seconds).
  6. Turn orders.readonly off.
  7. Watch the connection count recover to its normal level of about 400.

Verification

CheckExpected
Writer instanceorders-aurora-prod-r2

Rollback

If the new writer is unhealthy, fail back to orders-aurora-prod-r1 using the same steps above, including the replica lag check, and escalate to the on-call DBA lead.
