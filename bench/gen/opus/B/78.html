Who each plan suits

38

employees said in the pulse survey that they have children who will need braces soon. That is about a third of our 112 people, which is why orthodontia cover matters.

Basic$168 a year

People who want the lowest premium and expect only routine care. No orthodontia, the lowest annual maximum ($1,000) and the highest deductible ($75).

Plus$336 a year

People who want more cover than Basic, with a $1,500 annual maximum and a $50 deductible, but have no need for orthodontia.

Premier$492 a year

Most employees, and especially parents whose children will need braces: orthodontia at 50% up to $1,500 lifetime, a $2,500 annual maximum and a $25 deductible.

Family Max$756 a year

Families with ongoing orthodontia: a higher orthodontia lifetime maximum ($2,000), a $3,000 annual maximum and no deductible. It costs $264 a year more than Premier.

Recommendation

Make Premier the default plan.

  • It is the lowest-cost plan that covers orthodontia, which matters to the 38 employees whose children will need braces soon. Basic and Plus do not cover orthodontia at all.
  • Family Max costs $264 more a year and only pays off for families with ongoing orthodontia.
  • Premier costs an employee $41 a month, $492 a year.
