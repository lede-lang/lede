The network as a whole has 14,900 parcels a day of headroom, but it is unevenly spread. Memphis is over capacity by 2,200 parcels a day, and Tulsa, its nearest relief, has only 2,400 to spare.

Memphis and Tulsa plan

From 2026-11-23, reroute 2,200 Memphis parcels a day to Tulsa. That clears the Memphis overload but uses almost all of Tulsa's spare capacity.

Before reroute After reroute
  • Memphis: 57,200 forecast less 2,200 rerouted = 55,000, exactly 100.0% of capacity.
  • Tulsa: 39,600 forecast plus 2,200 rerouted = 41,800, 99.5% of capacity, leaving 200 parcels a day.
  • Both depots would be at their limit with no slack for the whole peak window.
  • Omaha could take up to 7,200 more parcels a day, but rerouting there adds a day of transit.
  • Seasonal hiring: 640 people in total, 210 of them for Memphis.

Risks

No slack at Memphis or Tulsa. Any outage or weather delay at either depot during the peak window has nowhere to go except Omaha, which adds a day of transit.
Staffing deadline. The plan relies on all 640 seasonal workers, including 210 for Memphis, starting by 2026-11-16, one week before the window opens.
