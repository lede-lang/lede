  • It has the highest net benefit: $5,025,000 over 25 years, $265,000 above SunHarvest.
  • It finishes earliest, on 2027-07-31, a month before the school year starts.
  • It is the largest system (1,250 kW) and carries a 25-year warranty.
The trade-off: Copperfield also has the highest upfront price, $2,875,000. That is $235,000 more than SunHarvest and $475,000 more than Meridian, and the district must finance it.
