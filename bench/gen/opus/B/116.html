Who can join the library?

Anyone who lives, works or studies in Millbrook County can join. Membership is free.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian needs to sign you up.

How many items can I borrow?

You can borrow up to 30 items at once. Within those 30:

  • up to 5 can be DVDs
  • up to 2 can be Wi-Fi hotspots

How long can I keep things?

ItemLoan period
Books and audiobooks21 days
Wi-Fi hotspots14 days
DVDs7 days

What if I return something late?

From 2026-11-01, there are no late fines. Just bring it back.

There are two things to know:

  • If an item is 30 days overdue, we treat it as lost. We then charge its replacement cost, up to a maximum of $25 an item.
  • If your account owes $10 or more, borrowing is paused until the balance drops below $10.

How do I renew?

You can renew online, in the app or at the desk. Each item can be renewed up to 3 times.

You can't renew an item if another reader is waiting for it.

How do I reserve an item?

Reservations are free. You can have up to 15 at once.

When your item is ready, we'll email you. It then waits for you at the desk for 7 days.

Still have a question?

Email help@millbrooklibrary.example, or talk to us at the desk on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00.
