Extended hours is the most expensive per contact, but it is the only option that treats patients needing a physical exam.

What each can and cannot do

Extended clinic hours

  • Handles any visit that needs an exam
  • Highest cost per contact (≈ $38)
  • Longest average wait (25 minutes)

Telehealth urgent care

  • About 1,500 visits a month
  • Average wait of 12 minutes
  • Cannot handle visits that need a physical exam

Nurse triage line

  • Lowest cost per contact (≈ $7)
  • Answered within 3 minutes, 17:00 to 23:00
  • Vendor estimates it avoids 18% of emergency room referrals
  • Phone advice, not a visit
