How much leave do I get?

If you are the primary caregiver, you get 16 weeks of leave at full pay. If you are the secondary caregiver, you get 8 weeks at full pay.

This is the same whether your family is growing through birth, adoption or foster placement.

Who is eligible?

You are eligible if you have worked at Tessellate for at least 6 months on the day your leave starts.

When can I start and finish?

You can start your leave up to 2 weeks before the expected date of birth or placement.

Your leave needs to be finished within 12 months of the birth or placement.

Can I split my leave?

Yes. You can take your leave in up to 2 blocks.

If you take a second block, agree it with your manager at least 4 weeks ahead.

What happens to my pay, benefits and equity?

  • Pay: your leave is at full pay.
  • Health insurance: continues, with Tessellate paying the normal employer contribution.
  • Equity: keeps vesting while you are on leave.

How do I come back?

You don't have to go straight back to a full week. For your first 4 weeks back, you can work a transition schedule of 80% time at 100% pay.

Talk to your manager about what that schedule looks like for you before you return.

How do I apply?

Tell your manager and the People Team at least 30 days ahead using the leave form in the HR portal. If your baby arrives early or a placement happens quickly, just let us know as soon as you can.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Email leave@tessellate.example or ask in #people-help.
