Lease or buy the new digital press?

The commercial print shop needs a new digital press for about 2.4 million pages a month. This page compares buying and leasing over four years.

Risks

If we buy

  • It ties up cash we need for the new bindery.The $412,000 purchase price is paid up front.
  • The resale value is a guess.The $349,600 net cost assumes we sell for $120,000. If the press resells for less than $102,400, buying ends up costing more than the lease.

If we lease

  • It locks us in for 48 months.We are committed to the payments for the full term.
  • The buyout price is fixed today.If we want to keep the press at the end, we already know it costs $95,000.

Recommendation

We recommend the lease.

To be clear about the cost: the lease is the more expensive option.

It costs $17,600 more over four years ($367,200 vs $349,600), about 5% more than buying.

We think that price is worth paying because:

  • It needs no large cash outlay: $15,300 deposit instead of $412,000, which keeps cash free for the new bindery.
  • Service is included in the monthly payment.
  • It does not depend on guessing the resale value four years from now.
  • We keep the option to own: the $95,000 buyout price is fixed today.
