Heat-wave demand emergencies

  • For: On-duty system operator
  • Version: 2026-10-10
  • Available supply: 5,200 MW
Never switch off critical sites

Hospitals, the 911 center and water pumping stations must never be switched off, at any stage, in any block. Confirm they are excluded before opening a single feeder.

When to use

!

Use this runbook when the day-ahead forecast or live demand reaches 92% of available supply (4,784 MW).

✓

A stage is declared by the Duty Director, never by a single operator.

Stage 192% · 4,784 MW
  • Issue a conservation alert by text and app to 380,000 customers.
  • Ask large industrial customers on interruptible contracts to get ready to reduce load on 30 minutes' notice.
  • Confirm standby generation at 3 peaking plants can start within 20 minutes.
Stage 297% · 5,044 MW
  • Call the demand response program (delivers about 210 MW).
  • Start buying emergency imports from the neighboring utility.
  • Brief the regulator by phone.
Stage 3100% · 5,200 MW
  • Begin rotating outages in blocks A to L, in order.
  • Each block is off for at most 60 minutes.
  • Never repeat a block before all twelve have been used.

Steps for rotating outages

  1. Get approval

    1. The Duty Director approves in the log.
    2. The regulator is told within 30 minutes.

  2. Protect critical sites

    1. Confirm that hospitals, the 911 center and water pumping stations are excluded.
    2. Check that the exclusion list was updated this month.

  3. Run the blocks

    1. Open the first block's feeders.
    2. Restore it after 60 minutes at most and open the next.
    3. Update the public outage map every 15 minutes.

  4. Stand down

    1. End rotating outages when demand stays below 95% for 60 minutes.
    2. Restore all blocks.
    3. File the event report within 24 hours.
