How to read your electricity bill

A plain guide to what you pay for and how to pay less.

Rates effective 2026-11-01

Your bill at a glance

Your residential bill is made of three charges, with sales tax added on top.

Service charge
$14.50 / month

Fixed. The same every month.

Delivery charge
$0.045 / kWh

For every kilowatt-hour you use.

Supply charge
$0.08 to $0.31 / kWh

Depends on when you use power.

Sales tax
5.2%

Applied to the total.

Your bill is due 21 days after the bill date.

What each charge means

Service charge: $14.50 a month
A fixed amount that covers keeping you connected. You pay it even if you use very little power.
Delivery charge: $0.045 per kWh
The cost of carrying power to your home. A kilowatt-hour (kWh) is the unit your use is measured in.
Supply charge: depends on the time
The cost of the power itself. It is $0.31 per kWh at peak times, $0.12 off-peak and $0.08 super off-peak. See the hours below.
Sales tax: 5.2%
Added to the total of your charges.
Late payment fee: 1.5%
If you haven't paid within 21 days of the bill date, 1.5% is added to the unpaid balance.
Budget billing
An option that spreads your year into 12 equal payments. We review the amount every 6 months.
CARE program discount: 20%
Income-qualified households can get 20% off. Enrolling takes about 10 minutes online.

Time-of-use hours

The supply charge changes with the time of day. Peak hours apply on weekdays only.

Peak $0.31 Off-peak $0.12 Super off-peak $0.08

Peak power costs more than 2.5 times as much as off-peak power ($0.31 against $0.12 per kWh). Moving use out of 16:00 to 21:00 on weekdays is the easiest way to save.

Ways to lower your bill

  • Run the dishwasher and laundry after 21:00, when off-peak prices start.
  • Precool your home before 16:00, then set the thermostat 2 degrees higher during peak hours.
  • Charge electric vehicles after midnight, in super off-peak hours.
  • Check if you qualify for CARE. Income-qualified households save 20%, and enrolling takes about 10 minutes online.
  • Try budget billing if you'd like the same payment every month. It doesn't lower the total, but it evens out the bills.
