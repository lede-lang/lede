What happened

At 09:12 UTC a Terraform change replaced the weighted DNS records for match.eu with a single record pointing at a load balancer that had already been decommissioned. Players in Europe resolving the matchmaking endpoint were sent to a dead target, and match creation failed for roughly a third of requests. The bad record was identified and restored by 10:11, but resolvers had cached the failed lookups: negative caching with a TTL of 3,600 seconds kept failures going for close to an hour after the fix, until success rate recovered above 99% at 11:04.

Action items

  • Require a plan review for all DNS changes.
  • Lower the negative cache TTL from 3,600 to 300 seconds.
  • Add synthetic matchmaking checks from three EU cities.
