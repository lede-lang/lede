Two clear messages. Manager feedback and willingness to recommend Northwind each rose 3 points, the biggest gains. Growth is the only statement that fell, down 3 points, and at 49% it is now our lowest score.

What we will do

Three commitments, in date order

  1. Starting 1 December 2026 Train 90 managers on giving feedback Building on this year’s 3-point gain in useful feedback from managers.
  2. By 28 February 2027 Publish the career framework So everyone can see how roles connect and what it takes to grow here — our lowest and only declining score.
  3. April 2027 Run a short pulse check A quick survey to see whether these changes are making a difference.
