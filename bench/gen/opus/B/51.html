Summary

A storage policy change was applied to restrict bucket access to our VPC endpoint. It worked as intended for traffic through the endpoint, but it also blocked uploads made with pre-signed URLs from agents' phones, which do not come through the VPC endpoint. From that point every mobile photo upload returned 403.

The incident was declared Sev2, the policy diff was identified as the cause, and a hotfix removed the deny statement. Uploads recovered at 12:07 UTC, 112 minutes after the change was applied. By then 8,340 listings were missing photos and support had received 412 tickets.

Why it was not caught

  • 1

    The change was tested only from the office network, where traffic does not depend on pre-signed URLs from phones.

  • 2

    Staging has no mobile upload test.

  • 3

    The policy linter does not model pre-signed URLs, so it could not flag the deny.

What went well

  • ✓

    Once declared, the incident had a clear commander within 17 minutes.

  • ✓

    The rollback itself took under 10 minutes once the cause was known.
