Replacing our home-grown login: build or buy?

Our home-grown login system has become a maintenance burden. We compared keeping the build in-house on open-source Keycloak (self-hosted) with buying Authly or Gatepost. We recommend Authly.

  • 250,000 monthly active learners
  • Growing about 40% a year
  • Decision: November 4, 2026

Risks

Keycloak (self-hosted)

  • Most expensive option at every size we priced.
  • Ties up about 1.5 engineers, so we keep much of the maintenance burden we are trying to shed.
  • No managed bot protection; we would build and run it ourselves.

Authly

  • Cost rises steeply with usage: $288,000 at 500k MAU, close to Keycloak's $330,000.
  • At 500k MAU it costs $96,000 a year more than Gatepost's base price.

Gatepost

  • Price steps up sharply above 400k MAU. At 40% growth a year we would pass that in roughly a year and a half.
  • Bot protection is an extra $18,000 a year.
  • EU residency in Frankfurt only on the highest plan.

Recommendation

Buy Authly.

Authly sits in the middle on cost ($151,000 a year at today's 250,000 MAU), but it is the only option that covers all three of our must-haves without add-ons:

  • bot protection,
  • adaptive MFA,
  • EU learner data kept in Frankfurt.

Revisit at 500,000 users. At about 40% growth a year we would reach that in roughly two years, when Authly's cost ($288,000 a year) approaches self-hosting.
