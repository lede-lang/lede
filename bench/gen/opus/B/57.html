Choosing a CRM for 140 seats

Risks

Pipeboard

The ERP connector is certified, but the vendor is a startup founded in 2019, so we are betting on a younger company.

Cardinal CRM

The best fit to our workflow, but it needs a 12-week implementation and an implementation partner.

Thistle

The cheapest, but its reporting is weak, and two of our dashboards would have to be rebuilt somewhere else.

Recommendation

Go with Pipeboard.

It has the highest weighted score (4.05), the strongest ERP integration, and sits in the middle on cost at $109,200 a year for 140 seats.
