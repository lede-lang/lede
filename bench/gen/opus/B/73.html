How a building permit application is routed

Every application follows the same path. Two questions decide which review it gets: the project's value and whether it is in a historic district.

  • Current as of2026-10-01
  • ForFront-counter staff and applicants

Routing flow

Reading the flow

  • Start: the office receives the application.
  • Question: follow Yes or No.
  • Review stage.
  • Heritage Board review: only for projects over $50,000 in a historic district. It comes before plan review.
  • Permit issued.
  • Returned: the submission was incomplete.

Projects worth $50,000 or less go to fast-track review whether or not they are in a historic district.
