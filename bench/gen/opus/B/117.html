Saying goodbye to Elm Street

Dear friends,

We have some sad news to share, and we wanted you to hear it from us directly.

What is changing

After 17 years, our Elm Street shop will close for good. Its last day of trading will be 2026-12-23.

We know that for many of you, Elm Street is where you found your first favorite book, or brought your children for story hour, or simply stopped in out of the rain. It has been the same for us.

Why

When the Elm Street lease renews on 2027-01-01, the rent will rise by 70 percent. We looked hard at the numbers, and we couldn't make them work.

We also looked at how you shop with us now. More than half of our sales happen at Harbor Road or online. So as a family, we chose to put our energy into the two places that are growing, rather than stretch ourselves too thin to keep all three going.

It was not an easy decision, and we want to be honest that it was ours.

What stays the same

  • Harbor Road, our online shop and our school book fairs all carry on as before.
  • The people. All 6 booksellers from Elm Street are moving to Harbor Road, so you will see the same faces.
  • Gift certificates and store credit are honored at Harbor Road and online until 2027-12-31.
  • Loyalty points move over automatically. There is nothing you need to do.

How to say goodbye

We would love to see you at Elm Street before we close.

From 2026-11-28Everything in the Elm Street shop is 20 percent off.
2026-12-19, 11:00Our last Saturday story hour.
2026-12-22, 17:00 to 20:00A farewell evening with the staff and local authors. Everyone is welcome.
2026-12-23Our last day of trading at Elm Street.

Thank you, truly, for every book you bought, every recommendation you shared, and every afternoon you spent with us on Elm Street. You made it what it was. We hope to keep seeing you at Harbor Road and online for many years to come.

With love and gratitude,

Marguerite Pell

Owner, Corbel & Finch Books
