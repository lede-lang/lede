Pros and cons

Datadog

Pros

  • Lowest setup effort
  • Excellent alerting and log search

Cons

  • Most expensive: $1.36M over three years
  • High lock-in

Grafana Cloud

Pros

  • Low setup effort
  • Cheapest over three years
  • Dashboards stay portable

Cons

  • Alerting and log search are good, not excellent
  • Medium lock-in

Self-hosted

Pros

  • Lowest license cost
  • Low lock-in

Cons

  • High setup effort and needs 1.5 engineers
  • Fair log search only

Recommendation

Choose Grafana Cloud.

It is the cheapest option once staff time is counted ($782K over three years against $1,004K self-hosted and $1,363K for Datadog), and it keeps our dashboards portable.

Note
  • The Datadog quote expires on 2026-11-30.
  • The Self-hosted figures assume we can hire 1.5 engineers. That hire is not yet approved.
