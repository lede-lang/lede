Rotating TLS certificates on the edge proxies

How to replace the certificates on all 18 edge proxies safely: canary first, then batches, with a rollback path at every stage.

Trigger
CERT_EXPIRY_30D
Never delete the old private key before step 6.Until the old certificate is revoked in step 6, its key is your only way back. Deleting it early removes rollback for the whole fleet.

When to rotate

Rotate when a certificate has 30 days or fewer left. The CERT_EXPIRY_30D alert announces this. The certificates cover 12 hostnames, and every one must be on the new certificate.

Before you start

  • Have the change record open. You will update it in step 6.
  • Confirm no deploy is running in #deploys.
  • Check that the internal CA is healthy.

Rotation steps do these in order

  1. Confirm expiry

    1. a
      Run this against the live certificate:
      openssl x509 -enddate -noout
    2. b
      Compare the end date with the alert.

  2. Request the new certificate

    1. a
      Run:
      certctl request --profile edge-prod
    2. b
      Check that the output lists all 12 hostnames.

  3. Stage it on the canary only: edge-canary-01

    1. a
      Copy the new certificate to /etc/edge/certs/next.
    2. b
      Reload the proxy.

  4. Verify the canary

    1. a
      This should show the new expiry date:
      openssl s_client -connect edge-canary-01:443
    2. b
      Handshake errors stay under 0.1% for 20 minutes.

  5. Roll out to the other 17 proxies

    Batches of 3, waiting 5 minutes between batches.

    Canary
    20 min
    Batch 1
    5 min

    5 min

    5 min

    5 min

    5 min
    Batch 6

    17 proxies in batches of 3 leaves 2 in the last batch.

  6. Revoke the old certificate after 48 hours

    Wait 48 hours after the rollout, then revoke the old certificate and record the revocation in the change record. The old private key may be removed only now.

Rollback

The previous certificate stays in /etc/edge/certs/previous for 7 days.

  1. Copy it back into place.
  2. Reload the proxy.
This only works while the old private key exists, which is why it must not be deleted before step 6.
