Overview

Turbine readings flow from each of the six farms into one central stream, where they are processed once. The processed output is then split into a fast store for dashboards and a cheap archive for audits.

If the uplink fails

Hard limit: 6 hours of buffer

The Edge Gateway buffers up to 6 hours of readings on disk and replays them in order when the link returns. After 6 hours, the oldest readings are dropped. An outage longer than that leaves a permanent gap in the data.

  1. Link dropsThe Edge Gateway keeps receiving 1 Hz readings and writes them to its local disk.
  2. Link returns within 6 hThe buffered readings are replayed in order into the Telemetry Stream. Nothing is lost.
  3. Outage passes 6 hThe buffer is full, so the oldest readings are dropped to make room. Those readings are never replayed.
