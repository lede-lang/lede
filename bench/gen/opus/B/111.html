Watch items

Decision needed

Furniture clearance. Furniture revenue fell 32.1%, from $530k in July to $360k in September, as patio season ended. Its share of monthly revenue dropped from 28.6% to 16.1%. It is the one category that needs a clearance decision.

  • Seasonal and holiday momentum. Revenue rose from $90k in July to $520k in September after autumn displays went up on 2026-09-08. Keep displays stocked through the season.
  • Plants remains the anchor. At $2,490k, Plants brought in 42.4% of Q3 revenue and grew from $760k in August to $910k in September.
  • Basket size is rising. The average basket grew from $38.40 in July to $42.70 in September.
