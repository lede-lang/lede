Real-time Quote Pricing Model

Orchard Insurance · ML Platform · Reviewed 2026-10-12

Overview

The quote pricing service scores insurance quotes in real time for agents. At peak it prices about 450 quotes per second, with a p99 latency target of 250 ms end to end. Models are trained and registered in the ML account, then promoted into the production account for serving.

Deployment

  1. Register the new model version.
  2. Approve it in Model Registry.
  3. Blue/green deploy to the SageMaker Endpoint, shifting 10% of traffic every 5 minutes.
  4. Roll back automatically if p99 latency exceeds 250 ms.
Feature Cache is not a source of truth On a cache miss the Inference Lambda falls back to the policy database, which adds about 90 ms to the request.
