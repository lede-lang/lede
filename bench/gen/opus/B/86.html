Brightwater Maritime Museum presents Tides of Trade

A new exhibition on the spice, tea and cotton routes of 1600 to 1850, opening 14 November 2026.

For immediate release

The Brightwater Maritime Museum invites the public to Tides of Trade, an exhibition of 140 objects from 12 countries that traces how spice, tea and cotton crossed the oceans between 1600 and 1850. Its centerpiece, the 1783 log of the brig Hesper, goes on public display for the first time.

About the exhibition

Tides of Trade follows three of the great commodity routes of the early modern world — spice, tea and cotton — across two and a half centuries, from 1600 to 1850.

  • Spice
  • Tea
  • Cotton
  • 1600–1850

Visitors meet the trade through the records and tools of the people who carried it: original ship ledgers, navigation instruments, and a full-size reconstruction of a cargo hold that lets visitors step inside the space where these goods crossed the sea.

Centerpiece

The 1783 log of the brig Hesper, shown publicly for the first time.
