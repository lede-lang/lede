Lantern Notes changelog

Lantern Notes 3.8.0

Released October 14, 2026

Shared folders, scheduled dark mode, Evernote import, and much faster search. Here is what's in this release.

New 3 features

  • Shared foldersInvite up to 25 collaborators to a folder, and choose view or edit access for each person.
  • Scheduled dark modeSwitch to dark mode automatically at sunset, or on a time range you set.
  • Import from EvernoteBring in your ENEX files, including attachments up to 50 MB each.

Improved Faster

  • Search is about twice as fastOn a library of 10,000 notes, the median query now takes 190 ms instead of 410 ms.
  • Better battery life on AndroidBackground sync uses about 18% less battery.

Fixed 6 fixes

  • Tables keep their column widths when exported to PDF.
  • The app no longer freezes when opening a note with more than 200 images.
  • Reminders fire at the right time after a daylight saving change.
  • Pasted web links keep their titles.
  • Dragging a note onto a sidebar folder now works with a trackpad on Windows.
  • Word count now includes text inside tables.

Known issues

  • Shared folders can't be used with end-to-end encrypted notebooks yet. This is planned for 3.9.
  • ENEX import drops nested tags beyond three levels.

Deprecations

  • Windows 8.1Support ends on January 31, 2027.
  • AndroidFrom version 3.9, Lantern Notes will require Android 10 or later.
