Managed Kafka, even though it costs $3,750 a month more than SQS, because 7-day replay is an audit requirement.

Risks

Managed Kafka

  • Main risk: little Kafka experience.Only 2 of our 9 engineers have run Kafka before. We budget 5 days of training.
  • Highest monthly cost.$5,850 a month, $3,750 more than SQS.

SQS with SNS

  • No replay.The audit requirement would depend on an archive we build and run ourselves.
  • Throughput cap.Each FIFO queue is capped near 3,000 msg/s, so 4,200 msg/s needs two queues.

RabbitMQ, self-run

  • Replay depends on an unfamiliar plugin.The streams plugin is the only route to replay, and the team has not used it.
  • We build the ordering.Per-account ordering relies on queue sharding we would write ourselves.
  • Most operations work.30 hours a month.

Recommendation

Choose Managed Kafka.

It costs $3,750 a month more than SQS: $70,200 a year against $25,200.
  • Replay of the last 7 days is an audit requirement, and Kafka is the only option where it is built in.
  • It meets per-account ordering through partition keys, with no extra queues or custom sharding.
  • SQS is cheaper but cannot replay; RabbitMQ could only replay through a plugin the team has never run, and needs 30 hours a month of operations.
Main risk: only 2 of our 9 engineers have run Kafka before. We budget 5 days of training.
