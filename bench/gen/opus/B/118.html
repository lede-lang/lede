Getting ready for your MRI scan

An MRI is safe and painless. Here is what to do before, during and after your scan.

Have a pacemaker or metal implant?

Call the imaging desk on extension 2210 at least 2 weeks before your scan.

This includes a pacemaker, a cochlear implant, an aneurysm clip or any other metal implant. Some devices need special planning, and your scan may need to be rescheduled. Please don't wait until the day.

1Before the day

  • Metal implants: if you have a pacemaker, cochlear implant, aneurysm clip or any other metal implant, call extension 2210 at least 2 weeks ahead.
  • Tell us if you might be pregnant or if you have kidney disease.
  • Uncomfortable in small spaces? Ask your doctor about a mild sedative. If you take one, arrange for someone to drive you home.
  • If your scan uses contrast dye, don't eat for 4 hours before. You can still drink water.

2On the day

  • Arrive 30 minutes early at the Imaging Department on level 2.
  • Bring a photo ID and your insurance details. If you were given a paper referral, bring that too.
  • You'll leave jewelry, watches, hearing aids and wallets in a locker.
  • Wear clothes without metal zippers or underwire.

3During the scan

  • The scan takes 30 to 60 minutes.
  • You lie on a padded bed that slides into a tube. The tube is open at both ends.
  • The machine is loud. We'll give you earplugs or headphones.
  • You need to keep very still.

You'll hold a squeeze ball the whole time. Squeeze it at any moment and the technologist will answer you.

4After the scan

  • You can eat, drive and go back to work straight away. If you took a sedative, don't drive; have your driver take you home.
  • If you had contrast dye, drink extra water.
  • Your doctor gets your results within 3 business days. They'll appear in the patient portal after your doctor has seen them.
