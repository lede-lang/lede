What changed

+0.7 pts

Personal loans

Highest delinquency in the portfolio and the largest rise, from 3.4% to 4.1%.

+0.3 pts

Auto loans

Rising more slowly, from 1.9% to 2.2%, but it matters more in dollars: at $412M, auto is almost three times the size of the personal book.

flat

Mortgages

Steady at 0.6%. Mortgages are 60.3% of balances, which keeps the portfolio rate low.

Charge-offs rose to $3.9M in Q3 from $3.1M in Q2. The allowance for loan losses stands at $21.3M, 1.3% of loans.

Actions

  1. Tighten personal loan underwriting for credit scores below 640.Starting 2026-11-15
  2. Add early outreach for auto loans at 15 days past due.To slow the rise before loans reach 30 days
  3. Review reserve adequacy of the $21.3M allowance.At the January board meeting
