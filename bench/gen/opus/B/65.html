Greenhouse gas inventory, 2023 to 2025

Our emissions fell by 13.7% over two years, mostly because of cleaner electricity. Most of what remains sits in our supply chain, and we are not yet on a path that reaches our 2030 target. All figures in tonnes of CO2e.

Scope 2 emissions fell 54.6% as we moved 6 of our 8 sites to renewable electricity contracts. That accounts for most of the reduction so far. Scope 3, the emissions in our value chain, now makes up 86.6% of the total, and that is the real challenge.

Our target is a 30% cut against 2023 by 2030, which means 18,550 tonnes or lower. From 2025 we still have 4,320 tonnes to go.

Scope 3 fell 1,600 tonnes, a smaller share than scopes 1 and 2, so its weight in the total has grown. Reaching 18,550 tonnes by 2030 will depend mainly on cutting scope 3.

Method and caveats

  • Scope 3 covers purchased materials, upstream transport and business travel only. It does not include the use of sold products.
  • The 2023 baseline was restated in 2025 after supplier data improved.
  • The inventory follows the GHG Protocol and has not yet been externally assured.
