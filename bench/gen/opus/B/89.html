Your bill has three parts: what you pay for the water you use, a sewer charge based on that, and a fixed service charge. Here is what each line means, with a real worked example.

Usage charges

We read your meter to see how many cubic meters (m³) of water you used. The price per m³ goes up in three steps, or “tiers”, as you use more. Everyone pays the lowest price for their first 30 m³.

Water usedPrice per m³
First 30 m³$1.85
Next 30 m³$2.60
Above 60 m³$3.90

The sewer charge

The sewer charge pays for taking used water away and cleaning it. It is always 80 percent of your usage charge. In the example, 80% of $180.30 is $144.24.

Because the sewer charge follows your usage charge, every cubic meter you save lowers both lines on your bill.

Fixed charges

The service charge is $38.40 per quarter. It stays the same no matter how much water you use, and it appears on every bill.
