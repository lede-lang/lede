Clickstream Data Platform

Harbor Media · Data Engineering · As of 2026-09-30

Overview

Our web and mobile apps emit about 1,800 million clickstream events per day. Those events stream through Kinesis and Firehose in the ingest account, land as Parquet in a data lake in the analytics account, and are cleaned nightly by EMR into curated tables. Analysts query the results in Redshift, mostly through Looker.

Known limits

  • Firehose buffering means data can arrive up to 5 minutes late.
  • The nightly ETL finishes around 03:40 UTC; curated tables are not fresh before then.
  • Redshift is a 4-node ra3.4xlarge cluster and queues queries above 50 concurrent users.
