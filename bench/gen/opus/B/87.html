Home-office stipend

Everything you need to know about getting your home workspace set up and paid for — no ticket required.

1Who is covered

You can use the stipend if both of these are true:

  • You work from home at least 3 days a week.
  • You have completed 90 days of service.

Part-time? You’re covered too. Part-time employees receive 50 percent of the amounts below.

2What you get

StipendFull-timePart-time
One-time setup$500$250
Internet and phone$40 / month$20 / month

The setup stipend can be used again after 3 years, so you can refresh worn-out equipment.

3What you can buy

Use your setup stipend for the things that make a good workspace:

  • Desk
  • Chair
  • Monitor
  • Keyboard
  • Headset
  • Lighting

4How to claim

  1. Spending over $250 on one purchase? Ask first.Get written approval from your manager before you buy.
  2. Buy your item and keep the receipt.You’ll need it to claim.
  3. Submit your receipts in the expense portal.Do this within 60 days of the purchase date.
  4. Get reimbursed.Your money arrives within 10 business days.

Don’t miss the 60-day window. Receipts need to be in the expense portal within 60 days of purchase.

5What is not covered

  • Gaming equipment
  • Furniture for non-work use
  • Software subscriptions
  • Anything the company already supplies
