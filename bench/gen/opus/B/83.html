Calder led in every cropThe wettest county, with 701 mm of rain, had the highest wheat, corn, soybean and barley yields.
Dunmore trailed in every cropThe driest county, with 489 mm, had the lowest yield in all four crops. Its wheat yield of 5.9 t/ha is 2.2 below Calder’s 8.1.

The pattern held in all four crops. Ranked from wettest to driest — Calder, Elmstead, Alder, Brennan, Dunmore — the counties fall in exactly the same order for wheat, corn, soybean and barley yield.

This survey shows that rainfall and yield moved together this season; soil, planting dates and other factors also differ between counties, so it does not on its own show how much of the gap rainfall caused.
