  • Completion is up 9 points in four terms, from 70.0% to 79.0%, while enrollment grew from 1,240 to 1,502.
  • The biggest single jump, 5 points, came in Fall 2025, when free tutoring for first-year students started on 2025-08-25.
  • Online courses still complete about 18 points below in-person ones, so online is the format with the most room to improve.

Notes

Completed means a passing grade
A student counts as completed only if they finished the course with a passing grade. Attendance alone does not count.
Who counts as enrolled
Students who withdrew before the census date, the 15th day of the term, are not counted as enrolled.
Scope
Figures cover students enrolled in online and blended courses.
