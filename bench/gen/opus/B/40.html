Staff password and multi-factor authentication FAQ

A new password and multi-factor authentication (MFA) policy for all staff accounts takes effect on 2026-11-03. This page answers the most common questions about what is changing and how to get help.

WarningIT will never ask for your password or your code by email or phone.

Why are we changing the policy?

Two phishing incidents in 2026 compromised staff mailboxes. Longer passwords and a second sign-in step make it much harder for a stolen password alone to give someone access.

What changes on November 3?

  • Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
  • Every staff account needs multi-factor authentication.

Do I have to change my password every 90 days?

No. We are removing forced rotation. We will only ask you to change your password after a suspected compromise.

What if I do not have a smartphone?

The help desk will issue you a hardware key at no cost.

What if I lose my phone or key?

Call the help desk at extension 4400 to get a temporary bypass code. The code is valid for 24 hours.

Who do I contact for help?

Contact the help desk at extension 4400. It is open 07:00 to 17:00 on school days.
