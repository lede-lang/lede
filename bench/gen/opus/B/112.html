Hills, Plains, Coastal

The system as a whole meets the regulator's target of 105 minutes for the period, at 99.6 minutes per customer. Three districts are above 105: Hills (176), Plains (131) and Coastal (112). Hills is the worst by a wide margin, 71 minutes over target.

Next steps

  1. Hills tree-trimming program. Approved to start on 2026-11-02, with a budget of $1.8 million covering 210 line miles. Hills has the highest outage minutes of any district, at 176.
  2. Plains and Coastal. Both are above the 105-minute target (131 and 112) and should be monitored through the end of the year.
  3. Metro. Its 49 minutes reflects underground cables, which are largely protected from storms and vegetation.
