Small runbook for the Edge team at Sycamore Travel: rotating the public TLS certificates on our load balancers by hand. Section titled "Certificates" with a table of columns Domain, Expires, Days left: www.sycamoretravel.example, 2026-11-14, 29; api.sycamoretravel.example, 2026-11-02, 17; partners.sycamoretravel.example, 2027-01-20, 96. Section titled "Steps" as a numbered list: request the new certificate in the certificate manager; validate it via the DNS record; wait for the status Issued; attach the new certificate to the listener on edge-alb-prod; test with an external checker; remove the old certificate after 24 hours. Add a warning callout before the attach step: never remove the old certificate first, because clients will get handshake errors until the new one is attached. Add a note at the end that the automated renewal job is broken until ticket EDGE-812 is fixed, which is why this is manual for now.
