Quick decision page for the Growth team at Pebble Bank: build or buy feature flags. Today we keep 140 flags in a config file and a deploy takes 40 minutes just to flip one. Options: LaunchDarkly, Unleash (self-hosted), In-house. Section titled "Comparison" as a table with columns Option, Annual cost, Setup time, Targeting rules, Audit log: LaunchDarkly, 54000 dollars, 1 week, advanced, yes; Unleash, 9000 dollars hosting, 3 weeks, good, yes; In-house, no license cost, 10 weeks, basic, would need building. Section titled "Recommendation": Unleash, in a callout, because it covers our targeting needs and its audit log satisfies the compliance team at a sixth of the cost of LaunchDarkly. Section titled "Risks" with a bulleted list: we own Unleash upgrades and uptime; none of the options has an SDK for our COBOL batch jobs.
