The Design Systems team at Fathom Bank wants a page stating our product design principles, to be linked from every design review template. Start with a short intro paragraph saying these principles were agreed in a workshop with 26 designers and engineers on 2026-09-17 and apply to all customer-facing apps. Then five principles, each as its own heading with a one-paragraph explanation and a short list with one "Do" and one "Avoid" example: "Clarity over cleverness" (do: label buttons with the action; avoid: icons without text for money movement), "Show the money" (do: show fees before confirmation; avoid: revealing fees on the final screen), "Forgive mistakes" (do: allow undo for 10 seconds after a transfer is scheduled; avoid: making cancellation a phone call), "Accessible by default" (do: meet a contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1; avoid: relying on color alone for status), "Calm, not urgent" (do: state plain deadlines; avoid: countdown timers that push decisions). End with a quote from the head of design, Ines Albrecht: "If a customer has to read it twice, we wrote it wrong." Add a note callout that disagreements with a principle go to the monthly design council, not to individual reviews.
