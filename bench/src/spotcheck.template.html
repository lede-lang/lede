Lede spot check

Twelve blind comparisons from the vocabulary v0.4 round (fresh tasks, three writer models). Both pages in each pair were written from the same brief by the same model, one as raw HTML and one in Lede, with sides randomized. Pick the better finished page, look at the desktop view (light) and the phone view (dark), then say what made the difference. The two questions under the buttons and the note are optional. Everything saves as you go and is compared with the model judge afterwards.
