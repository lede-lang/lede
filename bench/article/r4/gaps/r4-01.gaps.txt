Lede has no way to run a replay or a what-if model, so I simulated the four options by hand and wrote the results in as static data. The replay rests on a handling time (20 min per truck) that the log does not contain; I calibrated it so first come, first served matches the log, and said so on the page.
Cannot express an interactive comparison (drag a slider for handling time or operator count). Substituted a small sensitivity table and chart for handling time.
Cannot show the queue as a timeline of trucks waiting (range chart needs dates or numbers per truck and would be hard to read for 16 trucks x 5 scenarios). Substituted a line chart of waits by arrival and a heat table.
Numbers with thousands separators (loads like 1,240) cannot sit in chartable columns, so the log table quotes them as text and the chart datasets are separate and plain.
Costs for appointments, pre-registration and the gate screen were not in the brief; I wrote "Not priced" rather than invent figures. The log has no date, so the Tuesday to Thursday rush could not be isolated; flagged on the page.
