Lede cannot express anything interactive: no drag-to-reorder, no per-item status (full / partial with a typed amount / deferred / declined), no per-item note entry, no live committed and remaining totals, no rule-break highlighting that updates as choices change, and no copy-out button.
Substituted: a static worksheet. Staff order by score with precomputed running totals against $400,000 (a bar chart with a budget line), the point where money runs out, a table of the five conditional requests, one alternative scenario, and a plain-text decision sheet in a code fence the chair can copy and fill in for the clerk.
The running totals, "over by" figures, and the conditional-request total ($359,400) are my own arithmetic; which five requests count as conditional is my reading of the panel notes.
Inner double quotes in two project titles became curly quotes because the field syntax has no escape for them.
