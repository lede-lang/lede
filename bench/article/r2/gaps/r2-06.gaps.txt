Lede cannot animate anything, replay it, or give you controls to nudge timings, and the brief's main point was to watch and feel the animations. A page of static text cannot show feel at all.
Substituted: to-scale range charts of each timeline, a comparison table, and a JSON block of the final numbers. "Nudging" is done by editing the numbers in the code fence by hand, which changes nothing on the page and does not update the charts. The faster (75%) and slower (150%) variants are my own invention; the brief gave no values for them. Easing, tilt, scale and the colours are written as text only. Pulses per second were worked out by hand from the cycle times.
