Lede cannot show colour swatches, load Fraunces or Source Sans 3, or render real buttons, chips, form fields and banners with their states, so nothing is "seen in use". I substituted tables (hex values, type sizes, component specs), a bar chart of spacing steps, a bar chart plus table of contrast ratios, and cards for do and don't. The alert banner text and the chip amounts appear only as text. The 24 px logo clear space is a warning callout, not a drawing.
Contrast ratios were calculated by hand, not by tool. I also inferred the roles for each colour and type size, and the button label colour (Ink), because the brief gave none. Hover and disabled values were not supplied, so I flagged them as undefined.
